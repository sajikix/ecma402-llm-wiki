## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) (co-moderators). The notes file is named 2023-01-12 but its own title reads "2023-01-13"; the filename date is used here. Most of the meeting worked through open normative PRs and Stage 3 clean-up ahead of Stage 4 requests: `Intl.DurationFormat` kept `fractionalDigits` and, after its `formatToParts` PR (#126), plans to ask for Stage 4 at the next TC39 meeting, while NumberFormat v3 kept `approximatelySign` and its option-read order, and the exponent limit (#117) was settled as an optional `R(Number.MAX_VALUE)` bound. ## Naming of fractional options

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#131](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/131)

[USA](../../people/USA.md) noted `NumberFormat` has `minimumFractionDigits`/`maximumFractionDigits` while `DateTimeFormat` uses `fractionalSecondDigits`, and asked whether `Intl.DurationFormat` should use `fractionalDigits` or `fractionDigits`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s framing: "fractional" is an adjective describing seconds, whereas "fraction" is a noun, so `fractionalDigits` is closer to `fractionalSecondDigits` and [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md); [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) saw no near-term need for both spellings. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed closing the issue and keeping `fractionalDigits`. **Conclusion: close the issue.**

## Should "approximatelySign" be renamed to "approximateSign"?

- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#121](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/121)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) raised the `approximatelySign` `formatToParts` type as unusual next to the other `...Sign` values and wanted a conscious decision; [USA](../../people/USA.md) preferred `approximateSign`, and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) floated `aboutSign`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the name predates ECMA-402 in ICU and CLDR, and with the proposal at Stage 3 and shipped in browsers the status quo should stay since there is no clearly better alternative; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md), [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) agreed. **Conclusion: close the issue.**

## Can the order in which properties are read be more comprehensible?

- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#122](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/122)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) argued the option-read order is observable, has to be tested, and should be more predictable (alphabetical was only an example, later withdrawn; copying everything in insertion order was the more likely fix). [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said `NumberFormat` was never meant to read alphabetically and opposed that as a criterion. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) judged this "a ship that's already sailed" for NumberFormat v3: the current order follows dependencies, the only concrete tweak is moving `trailingZeroDisplay` below `roundingMode`, new APIs should aim for alphabetical order, and old APIs should stay as they are. **Conclusion: no change in NumberFormat v3; revisit the issue in the context of ECMA-402 at large, independently of NumberFormat v3.**

## Normative: Limit exponent of intl mathematical value

- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#117](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/pull/117)

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md) said the PR reflects web reality, where values beyond roughly 309 digits format as Infinity, and noted an exponent limit would matter for ICU4X. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) was more concerned about the maximum exponent than the digit count, wanted to land this week to reach Stage 4, and suggested letting implementations use the Infinity replacement beyond a bound, never throwing, with the comparison done in mathematical-value space; [USA](../../people/USA.md) noted `Number.MAX_VALUE` is a float, and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) warned that comparing against a string-based mathematical value needs precise parsing. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) stressed the limit is optional, and that making V8's current behaviour spec-compliant requires nothing from [FYT](../../people/FYT.md). **Conclusion: use `R(Number.MAX_VALUE)` as the (optional) limit; double check with Anba.**

## Should Intl APIs match Temporal interpretation of calendar and timeZone options? #741

- wiki: [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)
- proposal: [ecma402#741](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/741)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) explained that Intl `calendar` and `timeZone` options take strings while [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) also accepts objects and ISO strings, and proposed leaving Intl as is and revisiting about a year later once [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) reaches Stage 4. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) preferred no change, and if any then not in the performance-sensitive Intl constructors, to avoid inconsistency with 402. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said calendar and time zone are special only because [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) makes them so. **Conclusion: no change for now; revisit after [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) lands at Stage 4 (put in ES2024 and revisit then).**
