## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (co-moderator). The notes file is headed "2022-11-16", which is a typo; the filename date 2023-11-16 is used here. The meeting opened with the ICU 74 web-compat regression that led Firefox to back out its update, then spent most of its time preparing the November plenary: four Intl [DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) PRs (negative durations, sub-second `display: "always"`, `hours: "numeric"` output, Temporal.Duration limits) got TG2 agreement to go to TG1, and [BAN](../../people/BAN.md)'s currency-rounding PR #839 was cleared for TG1 with a request to settle option names. A proposed `"fractional"` style for [DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) was deferred to a v2, [FYT](../../people/FYT.md)'s `Intl.Locale` `firstDayOfWeek` fix was sent back for rework, and issues #830, #777 and #809 were closed out as no action. [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) attended but did not speak.

## Editor's Update

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) reported a quiet month: a PR landed to align list handling with ECMA-262 (iterating over elements), and a new PR refactors `PartitionPattern`.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) reported very active discussion on how to represent complex messages, error handling, and the shape of custom formatters. The working group is still aiming to finish by April or May of next year, which [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) called risky but was optimistic about. No relevant news from the W3C i18n group.

## Updates from Implementers

[DLM](../../people/DLM.md) said Firefox had to back out its ICU 74 update. The cause appears to be an ICU fallback-mechanism bug (dependency handling between root and locales) rather than an intended CLDR change; the most visible symptom was a date format heavily used on the web to generate ISO-8601 dates, and Rich Gillam was already fixing it on the ICU side. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted it probably affects other data too, mentioned a new data-driven test framework meant to catch such problems in alpha/beta/RC, and asked [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to wait for the ICU fix before shipping ICU 74. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) also raised that the web misuses that locale because 262 offers no proper alternative; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) suggested continuing that conversation in the [Stable Formatting](../../proposals/stable-formatting.md) context. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said there are changes to [Intl Locale Info](../../proposals/intl-locale-info.md) to be discussed later.

## hours: "numeric" w/ 0 in minutes and non 0 in seconds cause strange output. #170

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#170](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/170)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md)'s PR #180 picks the only output that avoids switching between list and digital formatting and avoids ambiguity. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) supported the behaviour but found this part of the spec confusing; [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) noted that "numeric" could in principle render as "2h 1m 59s" rather than 2-digit fields. [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) explained that minutes and seconds default to 2-digit when `hours` is `"numeric"`, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said a compact "2h1m59s" form could be added later once CLDR has prior art, and preferred to see the full editorial refactor land first. **Conclusion: TG2 agreement to bring to TG1.**

## Style "fractional"

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) proposed renaming the sub-second `"numeric"` style to `"fractional"` (meaning "append this to the last unit that didn't have fractional style"), which would also open the way to fractional minutes and hours in a v2. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) found the naming confusing (it is the seconds that carry a fraction, and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) preferred thinking in terms of seconds plus a precision, with `fractionalDigits`). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) concluded that the design space needs more exploration, so the current style options stay and this is revisited in [DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) v2 (which cannot start until v1 lands); `"fractional"` can still be used internally in the spec for readability.

## Digital duration format should probably be more lenient with data #161

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#161](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/161)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked whether a change that relaxes engine restrictions on digital-format output, without changing exceptions or default options, counts as normative and needs explicit TG1 approval. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said [DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) is hard to delegate to ICU given the spec's complexity. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s stated conclusion: bring it to plenary when ready, not necessarily in the November batch. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) then marked all known [DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) issues "consensus" and "needs tg1", which brings the known issue list for the proposal to an end.

## Locale with -u-fw- value other than 13 value will cause assertion #78

- wiki: [Intl Locale Info](../../proposals/intl-locale-info.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-locale-info#78](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-locale-info/issues/78)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) recapped that TC39 had asked for `firstDayOfWeek` to follow [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)'s numbers 1 to 7, and that the string form is now preferred again ([DE](../../people/DE.md) said this would not block the proposal at plenary). [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) realised the proposed PR was not good enough because the value should reflect the locale passed in. After discussion ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md) on avoiding a special case, [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) on being liberal in input and strict in output) the direction is to return strings while accepting the numeric 0 to 7 forms used elsewhere in JavaScript. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) still expects a 15-minute plenary item once the PR is fixed. **Conclusion: [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to update the PR and bring to TG1.**

## Consider making ResolveLocale return the normalized requested locale instead of the available locale #830

- proposal: [ecma402#830](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/830)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued that `resolvedOptions().locale` exposes the available-locales list, an implementation detail, and behaves differently from `supportedLocalesOf`. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) questioned the motivation and pointed out that unsupported or empty input resolves to a default locale, so it is not mere normalisation; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) wanted the two APIs to behave as similarly as possible but was fine with no change. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) felt exposing it was a mistake, but the web-compat risk and weak motivation do not justify changing. **Conclusion: No action for now. Keep issue for potential future discussion.**

## Support handling underscore as a Locale separator on the input #777

- proposal: [ecma402#777](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/777)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) argued for the status quo: the underscore is legacy in UTS 35 and not valid BCP 47. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) agreed accepting it would be low risk but saw no convincing evidence to change, and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) noted userland can do the conversion. [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) and [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) agreed. **Conclusion: No action for now. Keep issue for potential future discussion.**

## Normative: Use CLDR data for currency rounding and precision, allowing users to specify rounding based on cash denominations in common use #839

- proposal: [ecma402#839](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/839)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md)'s PR adds a `currencyPrecision` option (`"cash"` or `"financial"`; "financial" chosen over "accounting", which is already a formatting style) because ISO 4217 digits are far more than anyone uses for cash in some locales. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) leaned towards `currencyPrecision` and wanted a signal from implementers on whether this is big enough to need a full proposal; [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) would check ICU ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted ICU (C and C++) has long supported it and ICU4X should soon), and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) supported it. **Conclusion: Ask for TG1 consensus with the currently proposed option names. [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) to update the pull request to address [RGN](../../people/RGN.md)'s concerns about normative CLDR references if that has not already been done.**

## Get the formats for a given locale #809

- proposal: [ecma402#809](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/809)

The issue asks for the date format pattern (or label) of a locale. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) saw it as a wish to parse localized strings, which `formatToParts` and date field display names already support; CLDR no longer offers a "localized pattern" and English-specific pattern letters would not generalise. **Conclusion: Say we're not going to action this, and give advice on what to do in the short term.**
