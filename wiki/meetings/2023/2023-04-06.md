## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) (co-moderators). With the ES2023 cut of ECMA-402 approaching, TG2 dealt with the last NumberFormat v3 merge blockers and two normative PRs: reading date-time options only once in `DateTimeFormat` (#709) and the new `hourCycle` default logic (#758), both given TG2 agreement to go to TG1. A `resolvedOptions` property-order objection to NumberFormat v3 (#753) was resolved by landing the PR and revisiting the order in a follow-up. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md)'s Stage 0 `ZonedDateTimeFormat` proposal got TG2 approval for Stage 1. The rest of the call went to [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md): throwing for sub-second `"numeric"` with `"always"` (#139) and `roundingMode: "trunc"` (#146) were agreed, and the `fractionalDigits` default (#144) leaned toward "option 4" pending a PR. Attendees [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [CDA](../../people/CDA.md) did not speak.

## Editor's Update

[USA](../../people/USA.md) reported that #753 (NumberFormat v3 into ECMA-402) was close to merging, #766 (an editorial PR renaming all non-PascalCased fields and slots to PascalCase, a big diff) needed review, and that #758 was presented to plenary without going through TG2 first. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) had one open doubt on #753 about property ordering, which turned out to be a normative question because #753 is a Stage 4 proposal and TG2 still has to approve such changes as a PR. [USA](../../people/USA.md) had posted a test build of the current draft, approved by [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), to the ECMA-402 Matrix chat.

## MessageFormat Working Group

## NFv3 resolvedOptions

- proposal: [ecma402#753](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/753)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) proposed storing a new `[[RoundingPriority]]` slot only used by the `resolvedOptions` table, moving the special steps into initialization. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) explained that compact notation without an explicit rounding priority requires reporting the higher-precision rounding type to keep the round-trip invariant. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) (with [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)) argued for merging as is, opening a ticket, and revisiting after the proposal ships; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) said "I don't like it, but I can live with it" and approved the PR. The property order is observable through `Object.entries`, and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) found it awkward that `roundingPriority` sits at the end rather than with the other rounding options. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked to land the PR and follow up quickly with another PR. **Conclusion: Revisit the standalone PR when ready.**

## Normative: Read date-time options only once when creating DateTimeFormat objects #709

- proposal: [ecma402#709](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/709)

Since October TG2 had wanted to see the impact on test262; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) reported that the change only removes duplicated property reads in a few places ([FYT](../../people/FYT.md): only day and time-related options). [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) noted that it had never been raised to TG1, and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) supported the change but opposed including it in ES2023, planning to update V8 anyway. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [USA](../../people/USA.md), [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) each gave a +1 to sending it to TG1 as a good change. **Conclusion: TG2 agreement.**

## Normative: Change the hourCycle default logic #758

- proposal: [ecma402#758](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/758)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) proposed making the default `hourCycle` data-driven: two new internal slots in the locale data record the preference, and the default is derived from whether `h12` is turned off, instead of the current algorithm (no one uses `h24`; the US is `h11`, most of Europe `h23`, Japan `h12`). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) found this more flexible than the current algorithmic matching. [USA](../../people/USA.md) asked that the new slots be PascalCased from the start, which [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) accepted. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) explained the sequence (normative PR opened, test262 PR opened, normative PR merges, test262 merges); [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) took an action item to add tests despite little observable behaviour, and the change is to be presented to TG1 in May together with #709. **Conclusion: TG2 agreement.**

## ZonedDateTimeFormat

- proposal: [intl-zoneddatetimeformat](https://github.com/FrankYFTang/intl-zoneddatetimeformat)

Timeboxed to 15 minutes at [JGT](../../people/JGT.md)'s request. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md)'s Stage 0 proposal adds a new `Intl` object, similar to `DateTimeFormat` but without a time zone, that can only format [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) `ZonedDateTime` (including ranges and parts), since the [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) working group concluded that `DateTimeFormat` will not accept `ZonedDateTime`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) called the problem space worth exploring, with [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) unconvinced by the proposed shape; [USA](../../people/USA.md) suggested a formatter working for all [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) types instead of one special formatter. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) regretted that the whole mosaic of [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) formatters was never fully reviewed by TG2, and floated a PR that disallows other [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) objects as `DateTimeFormat.prototype.format` arguments while keeping `toLocaleString`. **Conclusion: Stage 1 approval from TG2.**

## fractionalDigits, (||milli|micro|nano)secondsStyle impact to number of fractional digits while style: "digital" #139

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#139](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/139)

Sub-second display is controlled by `fractionalDigits`, which conflicts with `millisecondsDisplay`, `microsecondsDisplay` and `nanosecondsDisplay` set to `"always"`. [USA](../../people/USA.md) proposed throwing a `RangeError` in `GetDurationUnitOptions` when a sub-second unit resolves to `"numeric"` style with `"always"` display; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) clarified that `"auto"` is not a problem and that `style: "digital"` is only one way to reach the conflict. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) was personally fine with it but deferred to [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) as first implementor and noted the high bar for Stage 3 changes. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) supported (five +1s counted), and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) noted that a `fractionalUnits` option (issue #65) could later lift the restriction. **Conclusion: Approval from TG2.**

## Set default digital number format to minFrac=0, maxFrac=9 #144

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#144](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/144)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) pointed out that the default `fractionalDigits` of 0 hides sub-second fields (output `0:00:01` instead of `0:00:01.23`); [USA](../../people/USA.md) said the default was changed from `undefined` to 0 without realising this side effect. Options discussed: (1) no change, (2) default maximum of 9, (3) drop `fractionalDigits` for NumberFormat-style minimum/maximum options, (4) default `undefined` resolving to minimum 0 and maximum 9 internally ([USA](../../people/USA.md)), and (5) an `"auto"` string instead of `undefined`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) preferred option 4 (a "scalpel", close to the older behaviour), because options 2 and 3 would leave `resolvedOptions` reporting a misleading `fractionalDigits` of 0. [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) were positive but wanted time to check the implementation and see a PR. **Conclusion: TG2 positive toward option 4; pending a PR.**

## Normative: Set rounding mode to "trunc" #146

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#146](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/pull/146)

The `NumberFormat` created for the fractional part should always truncate but does not set `roundingMode`; [USA](../../people/USA.md) called it an oversight like the other bugs of the session. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) pointed out that rounding up, e.g. 59 seconds 999 milliseconds, could propagate through all units, and [JGT](../../people/JGT.md) said not rounding up is appropriate for time units. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) chose `"trunc"` over floor because it works for negative values too, and noted the PR can only merge after NumberFormat v3 landed. [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) (LGTM), [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), [JGT](../../people/JGT.md), [USA](../../people/USA.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) agreed. Afterwards [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) were asked to check that nothing normative was merged without TG2 consensus before the May plenary, and the slides on the remaining editorial issues were deferred to the next meeting. **Conclusion: TG2 agreement.**
