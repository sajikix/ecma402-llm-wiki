## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) (co-moderators). TG2 approved the `[[CompactDisplay]]` slot for `Intl.PluralRules` (#1019), agreed to add a W3C i18n review request to its stage-advancement criteria (#1021), settled that `h24` should behave as an alias for `h23` regardless of what CLDR does (#1002), and agreed to close the lone `timeZoneName` and lone `era` PRs (#958, #957). The limits PR for Intl mathematical values (#1022) and the calendar-arithmetic PR for Intl Era Month Code (#69) were discussed without a decision. [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) spoke but is not in the attendee list; the notes' PCO and HSI are normalised here to [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md).

## Updates from the Editors

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) reported no major changes, a handful of editorial PRs, and recent activity on the Era and Month Codes proposal.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) is now officially chair of the MessageFormat WG. The WG finalised the spec version it considers fine for the upcoming CLDR release and submitted it to CLDR-TC for review. MessageFormat has separate `:unit` and `:percent` functions rather than the `style` option of `Intl.NumberFormat`, and its datetime formatting uses structured skeletons that limit nonsensical combinations, unlike the `Intl.DateTimeFormat` constructor.

## Updates from Implementers

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said most outstanding issues with [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) and intl-era-monthcode are resolved; three things remain (datetime arithmetic, shipping two copies of the TZDB, minor test262 failures), after which an intent to ship for [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) is on the horizon. [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) had few updates for JSC. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted MDN and implementations of the latest proposals are lagging.

## Updates from the W3C i18n Group

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wants more collaboration with the W3C i18n group on [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md), the `time` element and MessageFormat, ideally through a liaison who attends both groups' calls.

## Updates from ECMA Liaison

[AKI](../../people/AKI.md) welcomed collaboration with W3C: joint sessions are allowed, and having someone from TG2 at the W3C i18n calls and vice versa would help. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) supported at least one ad hoc joint meeting, noting calendar conflicts with regular attendance. [AKI](../../people/AKI.md) also described W3C's GitHub-label-based horizontal review system, in which reviews from other groups are requested but never block merging, and offered to help facilitate.

## Normative: Increase limits on Intl MV and explicitly limit significant digits #1022

- proposal: [ecma402#1022](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/1022)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s PR widens the spec-defined bounds on string input to `Intl.NumberFormat` from roughly 350 digits to 10,000, covering the full [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md) range, prompted by [WH](../../people/WH.md)'s earlier concern; the limits do not apply to BigInt. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) was fine with it but asked whether embedded engines might need a lower limit, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) agreed to seek input from Moddable (TG3 meets on the 20th). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) prefers spec-defined limits over implementation-defined ones. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) will return next month asking for TG2 approval and wants more implementer feedback.

## Normative: Add [[CompactDisplay]] slot to Intl.PluralRules #1019

- proposal: [ecma402#1019](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/1019)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md)'s PR passes compact display (short vs long) through to `PluralRules` methods, since plural selection can differ between them in some languages. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted no CLDR locale currently needs this data but implementations should be able to tailor it (for example in `fr`). [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked about a `style=percent` equivalent, which [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said belongs to a larger grammatical-inflection effort; an issue can track it. **Conclusion: Approved by [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md).**

## Use more specific calendar-dependent operations in Until, Add, and ToISO #69

- wiki: [Intl Era/Month Code](../../proposals/intl-era-month-code.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-era-monthcode#69](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-era-monthcode/pull/69)

The question was whether ECMA-402 should define a general, spec-level calendar arithmetic algorithm (add a year in arithmetical space, then apply the same constraint as `with`) or keep it implementation-defined. [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) argued TG2 lacks the calendar expertise and that algorithm steps risk edge-case errors, preferring prose with room for implementations. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [USA](../../people/USA.md) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) argued that no other body specifies this, that the algorithm is calendar-agnostic, and that leaving it undefined causes interop problems. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) asked that the RFC 7529 prior art be referenced, and [AKI](../../people/AKI.md) noted a separate standard or technical report is possible. No decision: the PR will be split so agreed pieces land, [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) will scrutinise the rest, and it returns next meeting.

## Add W3C i18n Review as a stage advancement requirement #1021

- proposal: [ecma402#1021](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/1021)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed requiring a W3C i18n review for TG2 stage advancement. After [AKI](../../people/AKI.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) worried about external blockers, the wording became that a review must have been requested at least 3 weeks before the TG2 meeting considering advancement, without requiring the review to be complete. [AKI](../../people/AKI.md) confirmed the Ecma secretariat does not need TG1 approval. It applies to proposals rather than PRs, at Stage 3 for existing proposals such as [Keep Trailing Zeros](../../proposals/intl-keep-trailing-zeros.md); [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md), not a TG2 proposal, would be a good test case for asking for a review. **Conclusion: Consent ([LAF](../../people/LAF.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md), [BAN](../../people/BAN.md)) to merge the PR; leave it open about a week for late comments.**

## Unicode is planning to deprecate H24. What should browsers do? #1002

- proposal: [ecma402#1002](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/1002)

`h24` (a 24-hour cycle counting 1 to 24) is used by no locale and CLDR plans to deprecate it. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) listed four options: fall back to the locale default, fall back to `h23`, keep supporting it, or decide based on usage. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [HJS](../../people/HJS.md), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred aliasing to `h23`, since authors intend a 24-hour clock and it needs no ongoing library support; [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) suggested at most a console warning, and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) noted this matches the earlier `islamic` warning precedent. **Conclusion: TG2 will alias `h24` to `h23` regardless of what CLDR decides; CLDR can decide separately, and whether the console warning should be normative is left for a future agenda item.**

## Normative: Don't add default formatting to lone timeZoneName #958

- proposal: [ecma402#958](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/958)

The conclusion drafted by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) last month was approved: the proposed behavior seems better, but TG2 is skeptical it would not break the web, so it intends to close the PR and explore other ways to support standalone time zone and era formatting. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [USA](../../people/USA.md), [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) agreed; the door stays open if someone does the usage research. **Conclusion: Agreement to close the PR and explore other avenues.**

## Normative: Don't add default formatting to lone era #957

- proposal: [ecma402#957](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/957)

The same conclusion was sought for the analogous `era` PR. [USA](../../people/USA.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) agreed. **Conclusion: Same as #958, agreement to close the PR and explore other avenues.**

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md)'s editorial PR folds the unsigned rounding mode into the regular rounding mode table; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) finds the separate concept useful to implementers and dislikes perpetually open PRs from this disagreement.
