## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (co-moderator). TG2 expressed support for advancing Intl Era Month Code to Stage 2.7 (pending PR reviews), [Keep Trailing Zeros](../../proposals/intl-keep-trailing-zeros.md), and Amount to Stage 2, and approved one normative PR (`%IntlSegmentsPrototype%[%Symbol.iterator%]` `name`). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s compromise for unshipping `islamic` and `islamic-rgsa` was accepted so the Era Month Code work can proceed, and the lone-`timeZoneName` PR (#958) is expected to be closed with a written conclusion at the next call.

## Updates from the Editors

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) reported purely editorial cleanup from Anba and that the normative note about browser locales depending on agent strings has landed. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) believed the GA approved the 2025 edition in Geneva at the end of June, which [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) thought likely.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) is taking over as chair of the working group from Addison Philips, who is stepping back but expected to keep participating. The group is working toward the CLDR 48 release and looking at percent and datetime formatting, where MessageFormat functions may diverge from JS `Intl`, for example a datetime approach closer to ICU4X's semantic skeletons. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) expects MessageFormat to remain a subset of what the JS formatters enable. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) will attend more regularly to help with cross-cutting issues.

## Updates from Implementers

[YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) had little to share. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted a few open PRs (numbering systems, plural rules) that need implementations to ship, and that the Stage 3 `Intl.LocaleInfo` looks almost ready.

## Updates from the W3C i18n Group

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wants more engagement with the group. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) noted there is little i18n activity in W3C for HTML and that a lack of interaction may be a good sign. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) mentioned hallway discussions at the Web Engines Hackfest about an HTML element for datetime (or message) formatting, with more ideas than proposals so far; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) noted customizable `select` has opened the door to elements that generate DOM content.

## Consider unshipping islamic and islamic-rgsa calendars #971

- proposal: [ecma402#971](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/971)

Continuing last month's `islamic` fallback debate. With [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) absent, [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed a stepping stone: make the fallback implementation-and-locale-dependent (ILD) but require a Temporal-supported calendar, the smallest spec change, leaving a stricter rule such as always `islamic-tbla` for a later normative PR; [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) had also suggested an error text or console warning. [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) accepted it (disliking that Temporal throws for these but seeing no alternative), and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) had no objection. [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) will update the proposal (likely removing rows from some tables), [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) will do a holistic review, and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) remains a Stage 2.7 reviewer. With no open issues in the milestone and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) planning an editor review, nobody voiced concern about asking for Stage 2.7 later that month.

## Keep trailing zeros in Intl.NumberFormat and Intl.PluralRules

- wiki: [Intl Keep Trailing Zeros](../../proposals/intl-keep-trailing-zeros.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-keep-trailing-zeros#4](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-keep-trailing-zeros/pull/4)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1hKJFrDfiGeqPWm51fQFQb4M4CeYm3ultB7Opef1BVuE/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) presented. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) questioned "decimal digits" versus "significant digits" (leading zeros are not preserved by parsing, which [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) considers a separate matter), the interaction with `roundingPriority` (none), and the meaning of `trailingZeroDisplay: "auto"` (unchanged; `stripIfInteger` strips everything). A possible third option, `stripFromString`, would recreate the current behavior and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) would add it only with a good reason. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) plan to review; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) voiced support for advancing to Stage 2 and/or 2.7.

## Proposal Measure for Stage 2

- proposal: [proposal-measure#34](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-measure/pull/34)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1my6X1ODDckzJmtcWcFI9hRF_I06Z4RQwrq81lbo8wPM/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) presented Amount (the proposal is to be renamed from "measure"), which reached Stage 1 in October 2024 and now seeks Stage 2 with reduced scope; it needs `Intl.NumberFormat` not to throw for `style: "currency"` without a constructor currency. Discussion covered rounding on the way in vs out (#35), where [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [JMN](../../people/JMN.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) favor rounding in the constructor with output such as "1.200", and treating currency (#36) as generic data with no rounding to currency precision in this version. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked for limits on Amount's mathematical values matching those of the IntlMV strings so implementations stay flexible; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked for an issue to include in the TG1 presentation and preferred a BigInt-derived limit, with [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) noting implementations have limits for good reason. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) supported advancing to Stage 2.

## Normative: Add missing name property for %IntlSegmentsPrototype%[%Symbol.iterator%] #1015

- proposal: [ecma402#1015](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/1015)

The spec omits the `name` of the iterator function on the segments prototype, although SpiderMonkey, V8 and GraalJS already match the PR; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) found that JSC deviates (returns `"anonymous"`). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) called it web reality and asked [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) to look into this before the TG1 meeting. **Conclusion: TG2 approval to move forward ([YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) +1).**

## Normative: Don't add default formatting to lone timeZoneName #958

- proposal: [ecma402#958](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/958)

[PFC](../../people/PFC.md) said the PR is not a high priority and closing it would be fine, since moving forward would need usage data. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) found the current behavior surprising but noted `era` has been omitted since version 1 of the spec, so it may be deliberate, and planned to ask early contributors. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) were unpersuaded that the existing behavior should change and are open to another way to format a standalone time zone name or era. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) drafted a conclusion: the proposed behavior seems better, but TG2 is skeptical that nobody depends on the old behavior and will explore other avenues for standalone time zone and era formatting. **Conclusion: Next TG2 call, we will approve [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s proposed conclusion.**
