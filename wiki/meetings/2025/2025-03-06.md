## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). TG2 reviewed [RGN](../../people/RGN.md)'s editorial `DurationFormat` clean-up (#972), which was headed for merge by the editors, and heard that the 2025 edition can be frozen soon. The rest of the meeting was dominated by calendars: [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) reported that Firefox may ship Temporal with a restricted calendar set, and TG2 discussed unshipping the `islamic` and `islamic-rgsa` calendars (#971) and what implementers need to ship Temporal (Intl Era and Month Codes). `Locale.prototype.getVariants` (#960) drew motivation questions. No formal decisions were recorded.

## Updates from the Editors

[USA](../../people/USA.md) reported that `DurationFormat` is merged, the 2025 edition has been cut, and the spec can be frozen sometime before ECMA-262 does so, so that both are released together. Editorial PRs are tagged as needing comments or consensus. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) considered #972 the more important of two pending PRs, while [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) also wanted the simplified `NumberFormat` PR in ES2025.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the group prepared for the CLDR 47.1 update, marking things "proposed" rather than "stable", and had a productive meeting with TG5 researchers whose report gave more confidence that MessageFormat achieves its goals. [USA](../../people/USA.md) described industry adoption work (React proofs of concept, Next.js applications, XLIFF integration) and suggested that once CLDR publishes, it may be a good time to present MessageFormat at plenary again.

## Updates from Implementers

[DLM](../../people/DLM.md) said Mozilla wants to ship Temporal soon but ICU4X and ICU4C disagree on some calendars, so they may ship only the unproblematic ones and restrict the rest; they also found no evidence that some CLDR calendars (e.g. the Islamic ones on the agenda) are used, while noting that lack of cultural knowledge may play a role. [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) added that ICU4X has problems for dates far from today. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) stressed that engines should ship consistent calendars that users actually use. Separately, [USA](../../people/USA.md) proposed moving old, finished items off the Status Wiki tracking page to an archive page, which was met with agreement.

## Editorial: Replace DurationFormat [[${Unit}{Style,Display}]] slots with [[${Unit}Options]] #972

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [ecma402#972](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/972)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) walked through the commits: typed records (`YearsOptions`, `MonthsOptions`, ...) replace the two per-unit slots, and a table-driven conversion column replaces special-casing in `resolvedOptions`, with no observable change. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked where the `~style+display~` spec enum is defined and suggested splitting the large table; [USA](../../people/USA.md) and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) agreed tables should ideally shrink. [USA](../../people/USA.md) suggested merging this as editors, with [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to review; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) had no strong opinion.

## Normative: Add Locale.prototype.getVariants #960

- proposal: [ecma402#960](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/960)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) said the PR is not ready for 2025; the open question is the return type and, if an object, the property ordering, since variants are non-scalar and need a method rather than a getter. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) called it a gap to fill, since every other subtag is accessible. [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) questioned the motivation, noting that the requester did not recall a concrete use case and asking how engine time is justified. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) noted that a getter implies a setter, since option bags do not accept variants. Open; the motivation and return type remain to be settled.

## Consider unshipping islamic and islamic-rgsa calendars #971

- proposal: [ecma402#971](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/971)

[DLM](../../people/DLM.md) noted that `islamic-rgsa` was requested by Oracle and is not implemented in ICU4C (it delegates to `islamic`) or ICU4X, and that `islamic` is observation-based with an unclear simulated location. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued for prioritising predictable, simulation-friendly calendars and preferring `islamic-rgsa` for naming its location, while [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) doubted the accuracy of the simulation and asked how users can map their needs to these identifiers; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked what "unship" even means when the spec does not define a shipping list. [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) wanted the difference between table-backed values and approximations to be observable in Temporal (which is not yet shipped), whereas [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) recalled an earlier long discussion that settled on silent fallback to an approximation, to be raised with the Temporal champions (proposal-temporal#2869). No decision.

## What do implementers need in order to ship Temporal? #21

- wiki: [Intl Era/Month Code](../../proposals/intl-era-month-code.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-era-monthcode#21](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-era-monthcode/issues/21)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) framed the discussion around the stalled Intl Era and Month Codes proposal (Stage 2, 15 open issues): what implementers need and who drives it. [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) doubted the era codes must be copied into ECMAScript and said computational details do not belong in the proposal, while [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted that engines should ship only useful eras (not ancient Japanese ones) and that `heisei 32` should map to `reiwa 1` rather than throw. [USA](../../people/USA.md) suggested starting conservatively and throwing outside a calendar-specific supported range. On ownership, [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) suggested Mozilla ([DLM](../../people/DLM.md)) drive it, and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) said they would discuss it internally. Open.
