## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). TG2 was positive about a locale-set privacy note (#780) and about [EAO](../../people/EAO.md)'s new [Intl Keep Trailing Zeros](../../proposals/intl-keep-trailing-zeros.md) proposal, with [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (explicitly for Stage 1) and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) voicing support. The long-running dispute over `toLocaleLowerCase`/`toLocaleUpperCase` locale resolution (#956) ended with [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) marking the PR as a draft, and `Locale.prototype.getVariants` (#960) was headed to TG1 as a PR. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md)'s proposal to stop falling back to LTR for text direction (Intl Locale Info #100) was supported. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) spoke but is not in the attendee list.

## Updates from the Editors

[USA](../../people/USA.md) reported a bugfix included in the 2025 edition, a markup change to the document layout, and an editorial change that marks the default locale as a fingerprinting vector; the latter is not yet on the 2025 branch, but [USA](../../people/USA.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) felt it could be included given the ECMA-262 precedent and that the opt-out period had started. Open items are naming conventions (blocked on ecmarkup) and simplifying `NumberFormat`, which needs reviews. None of the open items are urgent for ES2025.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) is actively participating and that the group is pursuing [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s recommendation for date-time formatting in MessageFormat to use a semantic skeleton or field approach (as in ICU4X) rather than the current `Intl.DateTimeFormat` options style. Percent formatting is also under discussion, either as a custom percent formatter or a scaling math operator. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) suggested prototyping semantic skeletons as a JS wrapper over `Intl.DateTimeFormat` (about a hundred lines) before deciding whether to add them to the Intl API; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) cautioned that a third options style could be confusing. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked about list formatting, which [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said should arrive in a future CLDR release (48 to 50).

## Updates from Implementers

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) had no updates. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) to look at missing test tick-boxes for JSC on the Status Wiki, and [USA](../../people/USA.md) raised whether to record engine version numbers there; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said that is best effort and a checkbox matters more.

## Updates from the W3C i18n Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said they and [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) have not been able to attend the W3C i18n meetings, which rarely cover relevant content. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said a liaison is valuable and offered to fill in until [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) returns, asking for volunteers.

## Normative: Added note about sets of locales for web browser implementations needing to not change as a result of user behaviour #780

- proposal: [ecma402#780](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/780)

[DLM](../../people/DLM.md) ran the text past SpiderMonkey's privacy group, who had no concerns. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) did not object but noted that limiting the set of supported locales would constrain a possible future in which browsers dynamically load data for the user's preferred locales (as revealed by `Accept-Language`); the current text leaves room for relaxation. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the text states web reality and the principles under which additional locales could be loaded, and that it is a net improvement since ECMA-402 does not currently say its APIs must not be a fingerprinting vector. [USA](../../people/USA.md) suggested seeking consensus at the next TG1 meeting.

## Intl Keep Trailing Zeros

- wiki: [Intl Keep Trailing Zeros](../../proposals/intl-keep-trailing-zeros.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-keep-trailing-zeros](https://github.com/eemeli/proposal-intl-keep-trailing-zeros)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) presented what is effectively a bug fix: numeric string inputs to `NumberFormat.format` and `PluralRules.select` would retain trailing zeros (`format("1.0")` gives "1.0"), interacting with the digit options as today (for example minimum/maximum fraction digits of 2 and 4 give "1.000", while 2 and 2 give "1.00"). This is done by changing the Intl mathematical value to carry trailing zeros, with a display option as a backup if it proves web-incompatible. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) hopes for co-champions and to present at the A Coruña plenary. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) expressed support, [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) explicitly for Stage 1, and said it does not directly affect the decimal/[amount](../../proposals/amount.md) proposals.

## Normative: Update String toLocale{Lower,Upper}Case to ResolveLocale with best-fit matching #956

- proposal: [ecma402#956](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/956)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md)'s PR resolves the locale list like `Collator` so `["unknown", "tr"]` reaches Turkish behaviour. [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) objected again that there is no case-mapping data distinguishing "en" from an unknown locale (only three special-cased languages in Unicode), and that this would burden implementers with no benefit for users, citing the W3C design principles; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) agreed and noted MDN documents that only the first entry is used. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) saw a real spec bug (and a footgun for developers) but [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred to first improve the Unicode-side data on which locales have case-mapping tailorings. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) concluded it is not worth further discussion unless such data becomes available and will mark the PR as a draft, taking it off the agenda.

## Normative: Add Locale.prototype.getVariants #960

- proposal: [ecma402#960](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/960)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) presented `getVariants` as the missing getter for the multi-valued variant subtags. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) agreed it is well motivated (examples such as `ca-ES-Valencia`), and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) reportedly does not oppose it. The shape was debated: [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) first preferred an array, but [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) argued for a `variants` accessor returning a canonicalised string, like the other subtag properties, and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) became comfortable with that. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) felt a new API should be a proposal (Stage 1 or 2 at A Coruña) rather than a normative PR, but [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) preferred a PR. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) will prepare it and seek TG1 consensus as a PR; TG1 will confirm [DLM](../../people/DLM.md)'s position.

## Henri may wish to discuss his explainer on unit preferences.

- proposal: [standard-measures-en-us](https://github.com/mozilla/explainers/blob/main/standard-measures-en-us.md)

[HJS](../../people/HJS.md) presented an explainer on giving users who choose `en-US` for language but live elsewhere standard measures (e.g. metric) via a separate identifier, rather than many parameters, crediting [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) for parts of the idea. [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) listed three risks: web compat is untested, the system settings that would trigger it are undecided, and it is unclear it works for things like date pickers, where metric regions still use different first days of week. Presented for feedback only.

## Text directionality should maybe not fall back to LTR #100

- proposal: [proposal-intl-locale-info#100](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-locale-info/issues/100)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) noted that some scripts are neither LTR nor RTL (traditional Mongolian is vertical), so a fallback to `ltr` is wrong, and proposed a normative PR that returns `undefined` when the direction is unknown. [USA](../../people/USA.md) preferred `null` but accepted `undefined`; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said top-to-bottom is well defined in CLDR, so all four values should be returned, after consulting Unicode about the naming. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) suggested presenting it at A Coruña as a PR.
