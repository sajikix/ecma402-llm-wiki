## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). TG2 agreed to merge the new `"tnsa"` numbering system PR (#614) and the `Intl.Segmenter` PR (#553) into the ES2022 edition, accepted [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s proposal to throw when `roundingIncrement` is used without matching minimum and maximum fraction digits, and closed the question of locale-customised `"min2"` grouping. Two `Intl.DurationFormat` issues were handled: undefined fields are treated as 0 (#90), and fractional hours and minutes (#89) were deferred to a possible v2. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md)'s request to advance Intl.Segmenter V2 to Stage 2 drew strong objections from [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) (formally on behalf of WebKit) and [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md), and ended with [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to take the feedback and decide how to proceed.

## Editor's Update

[USA](../../people/USA.md) reported that DisplayNames v2 and the TimeZone Names changes were merged into the spec, the Segmenter PR had been reviewed and would merge shortly, the numbering system addition was the important normative PR, and the ListFormat PR was stalled.

## MessageFormat Working Group

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md) presented the runtime-behaviour and case-selection documents and the 2022 roadmap of the MessageFormat Working Group; the ECMA-402 champions for this work are listed as [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md).

## Proposal Status Changes

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md) reported the status wiki was updated; major milestones are MDN pages for Intl.Segmenter and NumberFormat v3, and `Intl.DurationFormat` drafts for tests and MDN.

## Normative: Add new numbering system "tnsa"

- proposal: [ecma402#614](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/614)

The list of numbering systems in the spec was out of date; the PR (from Anba) adds the new system (the notes write `"tsna"` in the body but the PR title says `"tnsa"`). JSC had asked to wait until ICU shipped the system; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) and [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) confirmed ICU 70 has it. **Conclusion: [USA](../../people/USA.md): we can merge this one.**

## Normative: Add Intl.Segmenter #553

- proposal: [ecma402#553](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/553)

[USA](../../people/USA.md) was concerned that the Segmenter spec text was not consistent with the other spec sections and asked whether a restructuring would help implementers. [GPT](../../people/GPT.md) valued consistency but had no view on the specific change; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) preferred to land what exists given the publication deadline, since it only affects the location of a section. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said [RGN](../../people/RGN.md)'s suggestion would be ideal but not at the cost of missing the Ecma deadline. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked for contributing guidelines for spec authors; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) has long wanted this, the obstacle being what belongs in the spec versus alongside it, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) pointed to the existing style guide as the place to expand. **Conclusion: [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) to make the proposed change as a follow-up PR, and merge this into ES 2022.**

## Throw roundingIncrement if maximumFractionDigits is not 0 #81

- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#81](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/81)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) showed that `{ maximumFractionDigits: 2, roundingIncrement: 5 }` currently yields values such as 1.05, 1.1, ..., 2 and matching it would need an ICU change. Options: (1) require `minimumFractionDigits` = `maximumFractionDigits` and throw otherwise, (2) set the minimum automatically, (3) keep current behaviour and block on ICU. [GPT](../../people/GPT.md) preferred the stricter option since it can be relaxed later; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted exceptions are the most forward-compatible. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) confirmed the defaults stay unchanged. [GPT](../../people/GPT.md), [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) gave +1. **Conclusion: [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s proposal (throw if minimum != maximum, no change to defaults) accepted with full support.**

## Can locales customize grouping strategy min2? #77

- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#77](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/77)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred treating `"min2"` as a hint that obeys locale data rather than a requirement. [GPT](../../people/GPT.md) pointed out that a hint leaves the decision with translator data and a requirement leaves it with the developer; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) worried about resource fallback chains (e.g. `en-Deva-IN` vs `en-Deva` vs `en`) making a hint rarely meaningful. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) explained the motivation was predictability for test262, not a desire to change behaviour, and asked whether to keep the current behaviour. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) gave +1. **Conclusion: close the issue.**

## PartitionDurationFormatPattern need to handle undefined field in duration #90

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#90](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/90)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) proposed treating undefined values as 0. [USA](../../people/USA.md) offered two options: fix the bug that way, or convert the option bag to a full Duration object (which has zeros rather than undefined). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) and [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) gave +1 to the first. **Conclusion: treat undefined as 0.**

## How to format "0.50 days", "0.25 hours", or "3.25 minutes" #89

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#89](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/89)

[USA](../../people/USA.md) explained that fractional days are problematic because days are not always 24 hours (23 or 25 across DST boundaries), making arithmetic confusing, and that the omission was intentional to match the fractional sections display. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the days question does not belong here, but that fractional hours and minutes have use cases and the thorny parts (arithmetic behaviour, API shape, whether to stop at hours or include days) should not block `DurationFormat` v1, comparable to not blocking Segmenter on line breaking. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) offered "You have 2.5 minutes to complete this exercise." as a use case. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) (via chat) suggested proving that v2 fits as a forward-compatible extension before v1 progresses; [USA](../../people/USA.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) agreed a draft PR showing this would be good. **Conclusion: [USA](../../people/USA.md) will try to come up with a spec text modification to demonstrate this possibility.**

## Should we accept '\_' in identifiers?

- proposal: [test262#3173](https://github.com/tc39/test262/pull/3173/files)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md): UTS 35 accepts underscore in locale IDs, BCP 47 does not, and V8's behaviour does not match the spec. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wanted to be as strict as possible and throw when a string does not look like a calendar identifier. [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) distinguished (1) whether underscore is valid syntax (if not, it should throw, including for language identifiers) from (2) a syntactically valid identifier that is missing from older data, which should not throw. **Conclusion: [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md), [USA](../../people/USA.md) and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to follow up offline to correct this; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) can write the PR, which would be normative.**

## Intl Segmenter V2 for Stage 2

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1BJl99uYveimKrMw605KyaZ0qLthIhNaqONDPwdYH53A/edit#slide=id.g1085d0d2c96_0_204)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) presented line-break segmentation (and batch mode) for Stage 2. [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) said the group agrees on more than it disagrees (people need browser line breaking) but argued that capability is separable from this API: there are other shapes (content in a boundary shape, an iterator returning runs), the API needs font metrics to be useful, and it is the wrong abstraction level; canvas-formatted-text or Houdini would be a better home, duplicate APIs have a maintenance cost, and `v8BreakIterator` has shipped for ten years so there is no real deadline. [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) stated that the WebKit team would formally object to Stage 2. [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) (JSC) also opposed, saying it should live in a layout-related venue and that Node.js is a weak motivation. [GPT](../../people/GPT.md) (Mozilla) shared concerns about `v8BreakIterator` web compat and long-term cost and about users confusing "potential" line breaks with actual ones, while saying it should be done in the right place. [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) described a plain-text use case (bank terms and conditions); [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said measuring text in userland is much easier than break iteration and plugged ICU4X as a short-term option. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) argued users are already using `v8BreakIterator`, will misuse word break for line breaking, and that Houdini has not moved in three years. **Conclusion: [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to incorporate the feedback and decide how to move forward.**
