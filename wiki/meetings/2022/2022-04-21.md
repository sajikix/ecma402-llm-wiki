## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). [YSV](../../people/YSV.md) presented the TC39 alternative copyright license, and the status review covered the three Stage 4 items in the 2022 edition, polyfill and test262 gaps, and `Intl.DurationFormat` and Unit Conversion progress. Three proposal-level questions were discussed: the `eraDisplay` mechanism (TG2 chose internal slots), whether the Intl Enumeration calendar list should be specified in terms of `"gregory"` or `"iso8601"` (TG2 chose `"iso8601"` only), and the ordering of `CollationsOfLocale()` (taken offline). [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) spoke in the `eraDisplay` discussion but is not in the attendee list. In the notes `EOA` is [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), `LFA` is [LAF](../../people/LAF.md), and the Gregorian calendar is spelled "ISO-861" in places.

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) listed three Stage 4 items in the 2022 edition: Intl.Segmenter, Extend TimeZoneName Option and Intl.DisplayNames V2 ([FYT](../../people/FYT.md) confirmed Segmenter is merged; [USA](../../people/USA.md) that the other two appear merged). [USA](../../people/USA.md) noted that [LHO](../../people/LHO.md) (Long Ho) no longer works on polyfills (formatjs), so most Stage 3 proposals lack polyfills; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) expects ICU4X (compiled to WASM) to be a polyfill for many items by the end of 2022, while still seeing value in a JS polyfill during the transition, and took an action item to engage more non-implementers. On Stage 3 status, [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said Locale Info has one check mark for SpiderMonkey and NumberFormat v3 is implemented in JSC and SpiderMonkey with V8 in progress; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked for help with test coverage (format range, throw, mathematical values) and [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) said tests were being added and the PR should merge soon. On `Intl.DurationFormat`, [USA](../../people/USA.md) said spec work restarted, partial duration records were a spec bug with a fix PR posted, and nothing should block Stage 3; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) makes implementation difficult until it stabilises and DateTimeFormat changes are needed. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said there are no Stage 2 proposals; among Stage 1 proposals, Unit Conversion needs to answer questions raised by TG1 at the last plenary, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) would work with [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) on it. On `eraDisplay`, [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said it is mostly waiting on [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s time as co-champion, hoping to reach Stage 2 by July with no external dependencies known.

## Intl.DateTimeFormat.prototype.formatToParts ( date ) using eraDisplay - patterns or last time deletion

- wiki: [eraDisplay option for Intl.DateTimeFormat](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-eradisplay#4](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-eradisplay/issues/4)

[LAF](../../people/LAF.md) explained that with `eraDisplay` the era field may have to be suppressed at the last moment depending on the date, so two patterns (with and without era) are selected and one is chosen at format time. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked why this is externally visible; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) replied that the spec text has to reflect it even if the API does not change. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) compared it to [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)'s `DateTimeFormat` handling, which uses many internal slots, and said slots are acceptable for the few places where eras need them, without worrying about a slippery slope. [GPT](../../people/GPT.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) questioned whether piling on internal slots is the direction, and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) described the tension between i18n solutions and "pixel perfect" expectations. [USA](../../people/USA.md) asked that the duplication be avoided editorially and the dependency be flagged for reviewers; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the two proposals move in parallel and should be reconciled later. **Conclusion: use internal slots; try to make the mechanism consistent with [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) once one of the proposals is merged.**

## Special Tratement for “Gregory” calendar #5

- proposal: [proposal-intl-enumeration#45](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-enumeration/issues/45)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md): the Intl Enumeration proposal must include the Gregorian calendar, but [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) champions say ISO 8601 is more precise; options were (1) keep just `"gregory"`, (2) a normative change saying the list must include both, or (3) change Gregorian to `"iso8601"`. [USA](../../people/USA.md) said for practical purposes the two align but `"gregory"` is implementation-defined, and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) argued ISO 8601 is the specified reference calendar. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) disagreed that ISO 8601 is fully specified (e.g. week-of-year has no formula), and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) noted formatting and conception are real differences. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) pointed out `"gregory"` will be on the list in practice (alongside `"buddhist"` etc.), so the question is mostly editorial, and that `"gregory"` should not appear anywhere in ECMA-262. **Conclusion: use `"iso8601"` only.**
