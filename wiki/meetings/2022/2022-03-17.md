## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). TG2 settled three [NumberFormat v3](../../proposals/intl-numberformat-v3.md) and spec-hygiene questions (preserve web compatibility for `useGrouping: "true"`, error type for a `roundingIncrement` conflict, and disallowing `_` in calendar identifiers, which was also approved for the ES2022 cut). [LEO](../../people/LEO.md) started the process of cutting the ECMA-402 2022 edition. [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) presented the `Units Conversion Proposal`, which TG2 approved for Stage 1 despite discussion about splitting it between ECMA-262 and ECMA-402. The [Intl.Segmenter](../../proposals/intl-segmenter.md) handling of URLs, email addresses and acronyms (#656) was left for [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to investigate.

## Web compatibility: Allow the string "true" for "useGrouping"?

- wiki: [Intl.NumberFormat V3](../../proposals/intl-numberformat-v3.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#74](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/74)

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md): plain `NumberFormat` accepts the string `"true"` for `useGrouping`, but [NumberFormat v3](../../proposals/intl-numberformat-v3.md) does not, since `GetOption` was changed to `GetBooleanOption`; `"true"` corresponds to `"always"`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) stressed web compatibility is the biggest priority and noted the old behaviour coerced anything to a boolean, so the change should only affect the special string values. [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) observed that `"true"` is scarier than `"false"` since `"false"` previously behaved like boolean true. [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) said JSC supports preserving most compat cases by casting to boolean. **Conclusion: preserve most compat cases by looking for the exact string enum values and casting everything else to a boolean.**

## Should InitializeNumberFormat throw a TypeError when "roundingIncrement" isn't supported? #68

- wiki: [Intl.NumberFormat V3](../../proposals/intl-numberformat-v3.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#68](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/68)

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md): errors in [NFv3](../../proposals/intl-numberformat-v3.md) are inconsistent with `dateStyle`/`timeStyle` (RangeError vs TypeError). [USA](../../people/USA.md) thought different conditions can reasonably have different errors and floated a hypothetical third error type. [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) saw RangeError as appropriate when a value is out of bounds; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) argued RangeError is for single-value cases (out of bounds or not in a finite list) while a conflict between multiple options is better served by TypeError. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) agreed with [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), since a RangeError is more specific than a TypeError, and the group landed on RangeError, TypeError, RangeError for the three lines in question ([RCA](../../people/RCA.md) still saw the last as a RangeError, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted it is currently so because ICU supports nothing else). **Conclusion: change line 23 (the line in Andre's post) to TypeError; [USA](../../people/USA.md) to consider adding this to the ECMA-402 style guide.**

## Units Conversion Proposal

- proposal: [unit-conversion-proposal](https://github.com/younies/unit-conversion-proposal)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/18d1wh_hOolbmsIVQb5qGsv0-6EIWCZVrOhwRzZR9fTM/edit)

[YMD](../../people/YMD.md) presented locale-aware unit conversion. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md): the end goal is unit preferences; the proposal meets the Stage 1 bar. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) (via chat) supported Stage 1 but would be strongly against Stage 2 without substantial justification, rationale or changes. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) questioned why it belongs in ECMA-402 rather than ECMA-262, since only the choice of unit (e.g. for "person-height" in `en-US`) depends on locale; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said it looks like three APIs (locale-aware selection, mathematical conversion, number formatting) cobbled together. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) replied that the unit depends on the value (road distances in yards versus miles), so the parts are not a simple sum, and [USA](../../people/USA.md) noted the third part is unit formatting and that splitting would mean going Intl, then 262, then Intl again. [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) thought the proposal makes sense for the web platform and that user preferences can be added later; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) wanted preferences to be explicit input; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said TC39 plenary concerns were about having a cohesive story for user preferences. [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) confirmed the output is an array. **Conclusion: approved for Stage 1** (open questions to be resolved during Stage 1).

## Intl.Segmenter with URLs, email addresses, and acronyms

- wiki: [Intl.Segmenter](../../proposals/intl-segmenter.md)
- proposal: [ecma402#656](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/656)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) presented a behaviour difference between ICU and Chromium's tailored word breaking, suggesting an extra granularity (type `"token"`) rather than endless toggles; [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) thought it should start in Unicode/ICU and trickle down, or that Chrome could roll back its change to restore web compatibility, and noted Apple's DataDetectors as a separate way to find structured items. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) explained Chromium (Blink) patched break iterators years ago for URLs and email addresses; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) observed that ICU selects domain names as single words, so the deviation is only visible in Chrome. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s next steps: find out why the Chromium change was made, and take use cases to ICU/Unicode. **Conclusion: [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to investigate the V8 change; come back to this group if necessary.**
