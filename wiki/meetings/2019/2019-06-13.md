## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md). The notes have no title line; the filename date 2019-06-13 is used (the notes themselves name July 11 as the next meeting). Abbreviations are normalised to delegates.txt: SC → [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), FT → [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), LB → [LEO](../../people/LEO.md), SL → [SRL](../../people/SRL.md), CC → [CCN](../../people/CCN.md), RC → [RCA](../../people/RCA.md), FB → [FBN](../../people/FBN.md), EA → [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), `MM` → [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) and RG → [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) (in the Segmenter status line). Elango Cheran (`EC`, Google i18n) and Axel Hecht (`AH`) have no delegates.txt abbreviation, so they are named in the text and left out of `attendees`; `MN`, who speaks in several items, is not in the attendee list or delegates.txt and is kept as written. Most of the meeting went to normative PRs: consensus on #351 (`relatedYear`/`yearName`) and the content of the `dayPeriod` PR (#346), [LEO](../../people/LEO.md) merging BigInt support (#236) live, #352 closed in favour of Unified NumberFormat, and no consensus yet on `fractionalSecondDigits` (#347) or `quarter` (#345). The proposal status round was skipped, but its written updates record that `Intl.DisplayNames` was accepted at Stage 2 at TC39. The long discussion was `Intl.MessageFormat`, where TG2 compared existing libraries and ended without a formal conclusion but with a lean towards engaging Unicode on MessageFormat 2.0. A final item started the plan to retrofit `Intl.DateTimeFormat` for [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) types. Housekeeping: [LEO](../../people/LEO.md)'s suggestion that ECMA-402 be more welcoming became [ecma402#356](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/356), and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked for the next meeting time to be listed in the ecma402 README.

## Normative: Permit relatedYear and yearName in output (#351)

- proposal: [ecma402#351](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/351)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) explained that Gregorian patterns suffice for Gregorian, but the Chinese calendar has a 60-year cycle and related-year fields, so new `formatToParts` fields are needed; otherwise Gregorian fields would be reused for non-Gregorian calendars, which is confusing or wrong. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) named the fields `relatedYear` and `yearName`; [DE](../../people/DE.md) said the names are consistent with CLDR (and mused about kebab-case, which [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) pointed out cannot be used as object keys). [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) noted this is the field that is always Gregorian; [DE](../../people/DE.md) replied that the spec leaves it to the calendar system. [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) were fine with it. **Conclusion: ECMA-402 has consensus on the PR and the directly related PRs.**

## Normative: Add fractionalSecondDigits option (#347)

- proposal: [ecma402#347](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/347)

Already discussed at TC39 with consensus; partial tests are in test262#2194 and V8 has it behind a flag. `MN` raised that ICU's output for fractional minutes ends up as "29.5 minutes, 29.5 second" and asked how to propose fractional minutes. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) stressed that only a fractional second is added and minutes are untouched; [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) suggested an ICU bug with a test case, and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) thought `MN`'s proposal could extend this one later. Asked why `fractionalSecond` rather than millisecond, [DE](../../people/DE.md) answered that [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) supports sub-millisecond precision, and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said the earlier `millisecondDigits` name was replaced after naming feedback. **Conclusion: continue discussions offline.**

## Normative: Add dayPeriod option (#346)

- proposal: [ecma402#346](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/346)

Already discussed at TC39 with consensus. ## Normative: Add quarter option (#345)

- proposal: [ecma402#345](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/345)

Discussed at TC39 with consensus; V8 has it behind a flag. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) and [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) established that no spell-out data is needed (the strings are in CLDR). The debate was whether "quarter" is Gregorian-specific and whether to generalise to trimesters or other periods: [ZB](../../people/ZB.md), [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) and `MN` pointed to calendars and finance conventions that do not use quarters, [DE](../../people/DE.md) favoured keeping the quarter name and adding a new field later, [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said quarter and trimester coexist and cannot be merged into one field, and [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) saw a CLDR/ICU feature request and warned against designing an API without knowing its scope. [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) summarised two open questions: behaviour in non-Gregorian calendars and how to extend to trimesters and general periods. **Conclusion: none recorded; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wanted these questions answered offline and did not record consensus until they were.**

## Intl.MessageFormat

- wiki: [Intl.MessageFormat](../../proposals/intl-messageformat.md)

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md) presented a survey of existing approaches ([slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1RujNFCq3gH9TUEKDB_uFdKWNG1A1j2_NBCdnTmnEqv0/edit#slide=id.g5b6aec224a_1_72)), with comments from the room on each:

- **i18next**: a library that, per [RCA](../../people/RCA.md), just does quick key/value lookups (JSON, or plug-ins connecting to a database).
- **messageformat**: [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), a maintainer, noted it can also be used at compile time.
- **Fluent**: [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) gave two reasons for a custom syntax: localizers do not have to parse both JSON and message syntax, and under the principle of least power the syntax must be usable in a plain text editor. `MN` questioned who "localizers" are (non-technical translators who cannot edit such a file without training) and noted a front end could equally sit on JSON; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and Elango Cheran (Google) found Fluent more readable for plurals than ICU MessageFormat.
- **FBT**: [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) understood the point to be developers writing strings via a templating engine, with tooling converting them for translators.
- **goog.i18n**: no notes recorded beyond a remark that overloading is intentional.

In the open discussion [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) argued against standardising around something incomplete just because it is popular. `MN` proposed focusing on the data model (the user supplies the structure, the API formats, and serialization is left to others); [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) and [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) agreed, and [DE](../../people/DE.md) liked separating distribution from serialization. NC (Google's ARB format) and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) (escaping, message references, bundles versus individual messages) discussed the file-format side. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said "I think it would be a mistake for [Intl.MessageFormat](../../proposals/intl-messageformat.md) to implement ICU MessageFormat", but that Fluent's JavaScript implementation was "not ready for use yet as an Intl specification" (still 0.x, too few users outside Mozilla). [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) argued the division between code and data matters because translators are not technical; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) replied that assumptions like missing message IDs reflect MessageFormat thinking; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) suggested improving ICU MessageFormat to make it palatable for ECMAScript; [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) said not to focus on the translator's experience, which Axel Hecht disputed (a conversion step between programmer and translator adds complexity and slows the process); [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) wanted a focus on the developer ecosystem and tooling. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) proposed picking a champion. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) counter-proposed to start by engaging with the Unicode project on MessageFormat 2.0 and cooperate there; [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said that sounded good, and [DE](../../people/DE.md) suggested a separate sub-committee for the localization API, with enough interested people from both ICU and ECMA-402. **Conclusion: none formally recorded; the thread ends with agreement on [ZB](../../people/ZB.md)'s proposal to engage Unicode on MessageFormat 2.0.**

## Intl support for Temporal

- wiki: [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)
- proposal: [proposal-temporal#129](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-temporal/issues/129)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) described the state of the issue and asked whether everyone was happy retrofitting `Intl.DateTimeFormat` to support [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) types; [DE](../../people/DE.md) proposed working on the assumption that [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) is a global object. Someone (marked "`MM` (?)" in the notes) asked why anything else would be done, and [DE](../../people/DE.md) offered that [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) adds many new types and the logic might belong in its own class or module, which the same speaker found insufficient reason for a new constructor. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) noted `DateTimeFormat` is built for `Date` and [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) types do not have all the fields, which [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s table in the issue is meant to show. [DE](../../people/DE.md) would ask a colleague to write draft spec text.
