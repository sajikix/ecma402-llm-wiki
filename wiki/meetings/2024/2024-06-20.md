## Overview

A short four-person meeting co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). After brief status updates, the only substantive topic was the `eraDisplay` action items (proposal-intl-[eradisplay](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md)#9), where [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) asked how to move the CLDR-driven approach forward and described the idea of a "sub-era". No conclusion was recorded.

## Updates from the Editors

[USA](../../people/USA.md) reported a lot of recent editorial work that will not make it into ES 2024, and invited more editorial suggestions on improving the spec for readers, implementers and other groups. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked about the large editorial PR from Anba (updated by Ben) from the previous summer; [USA](../../people/USA.md) said the PRs appear to be merged.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[USA](../../people/USA.md) said the plan is to expand the set of MessageFormat stakeholders by inviting more people. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) pointed to an open issue with TG5 and asked whether the MessageFormat study there could be accelerated; [USA](../../people/USA.md) took an action item to look into it.

## eraDisplay Action Items

- wiki: [eraDisplay option for Intl.DateTimeFormat](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-eradisplay#9](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-eradisplay/issues/9)

[LAF](../../people/LAF.md) said the current direction is to have CLDR drive whether the era is displayed, which [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) considers a good idea, and asked whether other people should be brought in and whether a mock-up of the CLDR data would help; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) went through issue #9. [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) explained that in some calendars the era can be implied while in others (such as ordinary Gregorian) it must be stated for the date to be understood, and that historical applications may want modern ways of expressing old dates, so reckoning rules and era IDs should not be mixed up. A "sub-era" is when the reckoning rules do not change but the way of writing the date does, to avoid ambiguity. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked whether [era display](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md) would differ by language or region (Spain and Italy share the same change date) or depend only on the calendar system; [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) said it mainly concerns readers of another language in a region. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) replied that readers today are probably equivalent across languages in the contexts where they need disambiguation, even if contemporaries of the change date might have differed. No conclusion was recorded.
