## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (co-moderator). [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) and [JMN](../../people/JMN.md) announced the Stage 0 `proposal-numerics-with-precision`, and the group discussed it together with the `Measure` design for currency and unit inputs, with [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) favouring a single solution for both. TG2 approved normative PR #925 and approved #922 pending an [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) check, closed #608, and asked that `Intl.DurationFormat` return in October to verify Stage 4 readiness. In the MessageFormat update [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) agreed a 2.0 release this calendar year is not realistic for TG2's purposes. [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) is listed as an attendee but does not speak in the notes.

## Updates from the Editors

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) reported recent editorial merges, including Anba's abstraction of locale base name to make "language tag" clearer, and asked for reviews of several open PRs.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

A tech preview update lands in CLDR 46 with a syntax intended to be basically final (for example `.match` now operates on variables), and the stability policy is relaxed: no promise that a 2.0 parser can parse 1.2 syntax, replacing the reserved-content blocks. There is budgetary pressure to release MF2 this calendar year; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) called that timeline unrealistic and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) agreed, adding that the CLDR TC chair is asking for it. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wanted the TG5 user study to keep moving; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said its timeline may not finish before the 2.0 lock, so results would land in CLDR 46.1, with more scope for corrections thanks to the relaxed policy. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted a study result saying the syntax is good enough would itself be useful signal for TG1.

## Announcing proposal-numerics-with-precision

- proposal: [ecma402#926](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/926)

[NRO](../../people/NRO.md) and [JMN](../../people/JMN.md) announced a Stage 0 proposal: remove precision tracking from Decimal and add an object holding a Decimal or float together with its precision, which `Intl` objects such as `NumberFormat` and `PluralRules` could consume, likely via a symbol-based protocol and with no `Intl` API of its own. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) saw overlap with carrying a currency or unit with a number and preferred one solution, suggesting a constructor and significant digits over fractional digits. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked about precision propagation in math operations and text representation; [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) explained propagation is domain-specific and that `12E1` style notation expresses significant digits. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted that a combined numeric type with units, currencies and precision could serve many original Decimal use cases. Not advancing to Stage 1 for now.

## Design for currency and unit inputs that carry their values

- proposal: [ecma402#911](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/911)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) described a `Measure` object (a number plus unit, including mixed units) with `convert()` and `localeConvert()` methods, aimed at preventing abuse of `Intl.NumberFormat` for unit conversion, carrying units/currencies for formatting (e.g. MessageFormat), and now also precision. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) showed a constructor taking `unit`, `significantDigits` or `currency`, and suggested leaving `localeConvert` out initially. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) worried users would treat locale conversion as one-to-one and about currency conversion ([BAN](../../people/BAN.md): none planned); [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) said a `Measure` with no unit (CLDR's dimensionless unit) would make a separate number-with-precision type unnecessary. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) urged [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) and [JMN](../../people/JMN.md) to present this at TG1, and [JMN](../../people/JMN.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) compared it with Decimal128 on exactness and primitives. No formal conclusion; exploration to continue.

## DurationFormat for stage 4

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#209](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/209)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked whether JSC's implementation is complete or awaits more tests; [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) said it passes the tests at this point. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said Anba has tests in test262 and spec changes are waiting on new test262 tests, mainly test262 PR #3988. **Conclusion: wait for editorial changes and the last batch of tests to land, bring it back to TG2 in October to verify Stage 4 readiness, then ask TG1 for advancement in November.**

## Normative: Allow use of non-ISO 4217 data in CurrencyDigits AO #922

- proposal: [ecma402#922](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/922)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) found the PR good but wants verification that CLDR does not try to match ISO 4217. **Conclusion: TG2 approval, pending [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s query.**

## Normative: When formatting currency values, only use data on the number of minor units used to display that currency when using standard notation. #925

- proposal: [ecma402#925](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/925)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) judged this the right solution: `CurrencyDigits` should not apply in scientific or engineering notation, and already does not in compact notation. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) approved and asked about test coverage; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said tests can use currencies with well-defined fraction digits. **Conclusion: TG2 approval.**

## LookupMatcher should retain Unicode extension keywords in DefaultLocale #608

- proposal: [ecma402#608](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/608)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) questioned copying extensions from one locale to another. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) explained extensions are refinements that do not make sense on a different locale, and [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) agreed. **Conclusion: close the issue.**
