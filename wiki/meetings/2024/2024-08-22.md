## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (co-moderator). TG2 agreed a formal recommendation that a TG5 user study be completed before `Intl.MessageFormat` moves forward, and that the study's feedback be integrated into Unicode MessageFormat 2.0 before it graduates from tech preview. [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) presented several ecma402 PRs and issues (`numberingSystem` forwarding in `RelativeTimeFormat`, plural category sort order, inlining `GetOperands`), with `PluralRuleSelect` converging on taking only `[[FormattedString]]`. The Smart Units discussion shifted towards a `Measure`-like type that carries a value with its unit(s), and the `"und"` locale discussion ended without a conclusion but with a suggestion to handle it together with [Stable Formatting](../../proposals/stable-formatting.md) (null/`zxx` locale).

## Updates from the Editors

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) has been working through the issue backlog and asked whether older issues should be revived. Highlights among recent merges: #907 uses literal Unicode instead of HTML entities more consistently, plus other fixes to older issues.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) reported that the MFWG is taking feedback from the technology preview, with a work week starting 9 September aimed at resolving open issues and deciding whether to end the preview and effectively finalise MessageFormat 2.0 this fall. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked TG2 how it wants to communicate the readiness of the specification, given little feedback from implementers or localizers not previously engaged, and noted open questions about the built-in functions and their options (for example `hour12` vs `hourCycle`, and `select` values `exact`/`plural`/`ordinal` vs `Intl.PluralRules` `type`). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) raised semantic skeletons for `DateTimeFormat` as an option shape MF2 could recommend rather than classic skeletons; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) wanted MF2 not to require anything without an existing Stage 4 JS implementation and asked [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) to file an issue against both the MF repo and `Intl.DateTimeFormat`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) saw the TG5 study as a prerequisite and proposed a formal TG2 recommendation (see the next section).

## Updates from the W3C i18n Group

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) has attended the meetings without much direct relevance to TG2. Discussion of TG1's wish not to expose more through `Intl` than the user agent string exposes, and of loading additional locales on demand: [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) did not want designs to assume the Cantonese issue could not be solved by merging dictionaries, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the request accounts for timing side-channels but needs validation by a security team. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) favoured raising it with W3C privacy experts, possibly at TPAC.

## Updates from Implementers

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) reported that V8 is shipping `Intl.DurationFormat` (branch cut, expected to reach general users around late September). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked about `monthCode`/`errorCode` and V8's Temporal work, noting that the Temporal champions consider the spec stable and ready to implement; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) was sceptical that the spec had stopped changing, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) discussed how stable it really is.

## Recommendation about TG5 study and MessageFormat 2.0

- proposal: [tg5#3](https://github.com/tc39/tg5/issues/3)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued that the TG5 study is on the critical path for `Intl.MessageFormat` and should also gate MessageFormat 2.0 leaving tech preview, so that study feedback can still shape the syntax. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and ECH supported this ([EAO](../../people/EAO.md) noting the study's scope covers the syntax and should stay that way); the wording was refined to "graduating from tech preview". Action items: [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) to file an MF-repo issue with this position, and one on semantic skeletons. **Conclusion: recommendation agreed (LGTM: [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), ECH, [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [FYT](../../people/FYT.md)): TG2 requires a TG5 study to be complete before moving forward on `Intl.MessageFormat`, and recommends the study's feedback be integrated into Unicode MessageFormat 2.0 before it graduates from tech preview.**

## Inline GetOperands into PluralRuleSelect

- proposal: [ecma402#635](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/635)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) explained that `PluralRuleSelect` receives both the `GetOperands` record and the original number `n`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted ICU4X drops the `n` operand and that implementations always operate on the formatted string, so `n` should not be used. Rather than inlining, [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) proposed removing `GetOperands` and step 6 of `ResolvePlural` and passing only `[[FormattedString]]`; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) was tentatively in favour, and [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) is to make that PR for TG2 to seek consensus on.

## Smart Units Update

- proposal: [proposal-smart-unit-preferences](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-smart-unit-preferences)

After [BAN](../../people/BAN.md)'s presentation, [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) argued a units-conversion proposal for ECMA-262 should land in parallel to avoid `Intl` abuse, and offered to co-champion. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wanted it tightly scoped and suggested first pursuing a `Measure`-style type wrapping a value with its unit(s), including mixed units such as feet-and-inches, useful for MessageFormat as well, with Smart Units shelved for a year on top of it. [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) raised the en-US problem (many users pick en-US even where its unit conventions fit poorly), and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) has an action item to write something up on user preferences. Direction only; no formal conclusion.

## Intl.DateTimeFormat does not support 'und' locale #885

- proposal: [ecma402#885](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/885)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted formatters treat `"und"` like an undefined locale and fall back to the user's locale, and listed three possible behaviours: CLDR root formatting, ISO 8601, or the user locale. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) suggested defining a null/`zxx` locale with ISO 8601 date-time formatting (as [Stable Formatting](../../proposals/stable-formatting.md) proposes), with `"und"` possibly an alias. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) preferred `zxx` since UTS 35 and CLDR use `"und"` for root in a way BCP 47 does not, and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) said `"und"` may make sense for `Collator` but not `DateTimeFormat`. **Conclusion: none reached; address alongside [Stable Formatting](../../proposals/stable-formatting.md), and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) suggested seeking consensus on a Stage 1 update for it in a near-future TG2 call (possibly at the face-to-face).**
