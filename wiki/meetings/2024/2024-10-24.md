## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (co-moderator). A discussion-heavy meeting with no formal approvals: TG2 debated how `Intl.Segmenter` should report supported locales, reviewed 2024 accomplishments and brainstormed 2025 priorities (MessageFormat, Measure, RBNF, inflection/`DisplayContext`, dynamic data loading), and discussed [PFC](../../people/PFC.md)'s proposed guidelines for locale-sensitive tests in test262. NC (Google) joined briefly and is not in the attendee list, and Bob Jung (Google) also attended briefly.

## Updates from the Editors

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) reported a quiet couple of weeks apart from three normative PRs merged after TG1, and asked for reviews of their test262 PRs. [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) noted that proposal champions can help with the notifications.

## Updates from Implementers

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) reported activity on implementing `DurationFormat` in SpiderMonkey and said `Intl.LocaleInfo` is fairly ready to ship once engines add it.

## Updates from the W3C i18n Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) presented MessageFormat v2 to the WebExtensions community group at TPAC and got some support. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted a W3C AI translation API proposal that raises language-mapping questions (e.g. accepting `ja-JP` to `en-RU` without data for `en-RU`), and discussed input elements for currency or measures, which [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) said belongs to WHATWG. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wants TG2 to engage more with these groups, including user-preferences and world-locale work, and may take a more active role in 2025. The numbering-system PR (#929) was deferred until [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) could join.

## Consider relaxing locale resolution for Intl.Segmenter #895

- proposal: [ecma402#895](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/895)

`Intl.Segmenter` is about scripts and character properties rather than locale data, so returning the locales ICU is built with (what Chrome and Firefox do) says little about what it can actually segment; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) called the status quo simply wrong (e.g. Cantonese input is segmented as Mandarin). [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) preferred writing down current web reality unless [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) wanted to risk web-compat changes, and suggested sourcing a Cantonese dictionary for ICU instead of overdesigning. The discussion widened to the use case for `supportedLocalesOf`: [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) recalled the original introspection and fingerprinting motivation, argued the output should not be normalised, and thought Segmenter/PluralRules should accept everything. No decision; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) will take the use-case questions back to the CLDR working group.

## 2024 Accomplishments

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) summarised the year: monthly meetings averaging about 15 attendees, `DurationFormat` and `Intl.LocaleInfo` at Stage 3 and nearing Stage 4 thanks to [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), MessageFormat progress from [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), and new Stage 1 work on [smart units](../../proposals/smart-unit-preferences.md), Measure and conversion. NC asked that the impact of these proposals be listed as well, and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) asked about typed values (which [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) said is Measure).

## 2025 Planning

Candidate topics listed: `Intl.MessageFormat`, [smart units](../../proposals/smart-unit-preferences.md), Measure, sequence unit formatting (#398), RBNF (#494), Intl data loading (#434), `DisplayContext` (#355) and title casing (#294). The items below were then discussed one by one.

## Measure

- proposal: [ecma402#911](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/911)

[ZB](../../people/ZB.md) argued Measure is an ECMA-262 concern rather than 402. [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) replied that apart from a `localeConvert` method it is entirely 262, and was meant to give 262 users conversions so they are not tempted to abuse i18n APIs. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) added that messages need to know currencies and that skipping unit conversion in 262 would push people to misuse 402 tools; the 262/402 split can be revisited later.

## MessageFormat

- proposal: [ecma402#92](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/92)

NC called MessageFormat adoption a P0 priority, and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) said ecosystems such as React are waiting on it. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said the JavaScript implementation is about 7 kB minified and compressed and the main blocker is TG1 acceptance to move `Intl.MessageFormat` past Stage 1; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) described the chicken-and-egg problem and the TG5 user study plus [EAO](../../people/EAO.md)'s work as the evidence being gathered. NC said ICU 77 will ship MF2 as a draft in March. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked TG2 to explicitly endorse the spec once the MessageFormat WG finalises it.

## DisplayContext

- proposal: [ecma402#355](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/355)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) surveyed whether this backlog item should be prioritised. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and NC pointed out that inflection data is megabytes per locale (CLDR also has inflected units), so it depends on solving data loading, and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) expected it to be years away. No prioritisation decision.

## RBNF

- proposal: [ecma402#494](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/494)

[ZB](../../people/ZB.md) argued against RBNF over payload size concerns, while [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said RBNF for one locale is one data file and that they mainly mean digit-ordinals, not spellout. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) noted that solving data loading would free up space and that RBNF is just the mechanism for ordinals. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) pointed out that CSS counter styles already ship many of these counters, which [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said largely addresses [ZB](../../people/ZB.md)'s concern, though [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) noted Gecko implements them separately from ICU. No decision; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the case for why it matters still has to be made.

## Dynamic Data Loading

- proposal: [ecma402#210](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/210)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) laid out three options for locale data in browsers: the status quo, an async load API (best experience but privacy pushback), or a standard "language pack" format pushing privacy to the client, which would add another DSL. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) suggested tying deeper functionality to the locales a browser already advertises in `Accept-Language`, and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) warned this must not remove small data such as Cherokee collation. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wants invariants rewritten so engines can decide what is best for their users. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) also said title casing would need little data and that they would support a proposal but not drive one.

## Guidelines for locale-sensitive testing in Test262

- slide: [link](https://ptomato.name/talks/tc39tg2-2024-10)

[PFC](../../people/PFC.md) (speaking as a test262 maintainer, recorded as "PCO" in the notes) presented approaches for testing implementation-and-locale-defined behaviour without breaking on data updates: golden output, mini-implementations, stable substrings, comparative testing and metamorphic testing. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) pointed to ECH's work on adding test data to CLDR, and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) cautioned against CLDR as a moving target while supporting selective use of invariants (skipping locales such as Finnish where inflection defeats a test). [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) questioned whether test262 should check content at all, while [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued that exercising option code paths and checking sensible output has value. Open; no formal conclusion.
