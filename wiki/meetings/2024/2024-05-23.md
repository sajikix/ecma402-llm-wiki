## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (co-moderator). TG2 gave Apple's tentative feedback on reducing `Accept-Language` to a single entry, spent most of the meeting on the direction of Smart Unit Preferences (unit conversion inside or outside ECMA-402, "black box" versus "building blocks"), and discussed the `onError` parameter of `Intl.MessageFormat` and locale-sensitive substring search for `Intl.Collator`. No formal Conclusion was recorded for any topic; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted there is no TG2 recommendation yet on black box versus building blocks and planned to post a conclusion on the Collator issue (#506) and to ask TG1 for a dedicated 45-60 minute unit-formatting discussion. [CDA](../../people/CDA.md) is listed as an attendee but does not speak in the notes.

## Updates from the Editors

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) reported that several editorial PRs fixing long-standing issues have been merged, and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) said they are happy with the editorial-cleanup mode. Two large but good PRs from Anba (#826, #827) date from last summer, and [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) would like to put up a new PR with those changes.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said the WG is considering loosening the strict reading of the MF2 spec that APIs must always return lists of errors. Options include `.format()` / `.formatToParts()` returning an object or tuple for errors, or four abstract operations including `.formatFallback()` and `.formatToPartsFallback()`; discussion is ongoing.

## Updates from the W3C i18n Group

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) had hoped to bring the MF2 discussion to the W3C i18n WG.

## I18N objections to reducing accept-language #10

- proposal: [accept-language#10](https://github.com/Tanych/accept-language/issues/10)

[YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) relayed feedback from Apple i18n and privacy colleagues: there is no formal position, but reducing `Accept-Language` to a single item is probably the wrong trade-off, since sites need to know e.g. whether a Han-script reader prefers Japanese or Chinese. [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) said Apple currently sends up to two languages (the most preferred plus the system display language). [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) asked that the Safari mechanism be publicly described in the threads. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the group would continue the discussion and bring major updates back to the agenda.

## Smart Unit Preferences

- proposal: [proposal-smart-unit-preferences](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-smart-unit-preferences)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/5wv2lK6Fp#/)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) presented. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) argued unit conversion is a TC39/ECMA-262 matter rather than a `NumberFormat` option, and that a unit is a quantity of the value rather than a formatting option, suggesting a "measured unit" or compound-value input that `NumberFormat` could accept. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued for a black box (units are l10n approximations, not scientific conversion, and building blocks are easy to misuse), noting the conversion factors are about 8 kB; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) and SBE stressed that people will use any exposed output as a conversion API and that a black box alone would be abused. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s plan: gather pros and cons, ask TG1 for 45-60 minutes, and consider less controversial sub-proposals such as mixed-unit (sequence) formatting and `notation: "compact"` with units. **No TG2 recommendation yet on building blocks versus black box; one will be needed before asking for Stage 2.**

## Please remove onError parameter from format/formatToParts

- proposal: [proposal-intl-messageformat#58](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-messageformat/issues/58)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) explained that the MF2 WG is deciding how strictly failures must be handled, and that [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) favours the current `onError` callback as the most JavaScript-idiomatic side channel, drawing on the clunky tuple-return experience with Fluent. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) agreed error access is important but was not convinced `onError` is idiomatic, suggesting exceptions or an upgraded return type such as `FormattedMessage` with getters (like `RegExp` match results), and argued the object-count concern is small compared with Temporal. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) noted TG1 had earlier pushed the API away from a message-value return type. Left open: [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) may put the API shape on the TG1 agenda, since [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) lacks the bandwidth to do so.

## Add locale sensitive substring matching functions to Intl.Collator

- proposal: [ecma402#506](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/506)

[HJS](../../people/HJS.md) argued that collator-based search is needlessly complex (collation elements must be mapped back to text, Thai reordering and search-root contractions, three data layers with duplicated tailorings) compared with Firefox's precomputed case-fold and base-letter approach, which is faster but has no locale-specific exceptions. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) accepted the use case has many upvotes but concluded the problem should first be solved in Unicode/CLDR and ICU4X (possibly upstreaming the Mozilla approach) before ECMA-402. No decision was taken in the meeting; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said they would post their conclusion on #506 and put sequence-unit formatting and the `und` locale (relevant to [Stable Formatting](../../proposals/stable-formatting.md)) on next month's agenda.
