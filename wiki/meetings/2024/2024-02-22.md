## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) (co-moderators). A discussion-heavy meeting with no formal approvals or recorded conclusions. TG2 debated whether `Intl.MessageFormat` should drop its string-syntax parser and advance with only a data model ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) sceptical; to be revisited next month), explored a `null`/`zxx` locale for [Stable Formatting](../../proposals/stable-formatting.md), and gave [JMN](../../people/JMN.md) guidance on integrating Decimal into `Intl.NumberFormat` (carry Decimal128 precision through the pipeline). It also covered the Era Display data-driven `auto` behaviour, a Locale Preferences status update, and an editorial rename of `dataLocaleData` aliases. `HSI` in the notes is normalised to [HJS](../../people/HJS.md).

## Updates from the Editors

[USA](../../people/USA.md) reported that the repo has entered the period for the next edition of ECMA-402 and that [CDA](../../people/CDA.md) moved the default branch from `master` to `main`. Merged editorial PRs include updated references (#867), regularised `DateTimeFormat` table iteration language, and removal of an unnecessary key lowercasing step (#848); large editorial PRs still need review, as do #863 (add license) and #854 (publish biblio). [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) noted the group is in the 2024 opt-out period, so only editorial changes are accepted; the new tooling will automatically include the ECMA-262 bibliography. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [CDA](../../people/CDA.md) clarified that PRs from forks do not need rebasing.

## Updates from the MessageFormat Working Group

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) reported that the WG spent the previous week in daily calls to get the spec into shape for LDML/CLDR 45 (due in April), and that the final changes were being locked in that week. Changes include dropping `:plural` in favour of `:number` (with `:integer` as a variant), and leaving compound-value formatting options (number with currency or unit, date/time with time zone) out of the default required set. [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) asked about date/time literal formats; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said the WG chair was discussing whether to base the literal syntax on RFC 3339, XML Schema or their overlap, and asked for comments on that issue ([HJS](../../people/HJS.md) suggested the HTML syntax as a closer web-platform fit).

## MessageFormat: Drop string source support #47

- wiki: [Intl.MessageFormat](../../proposals/intl-messageformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-messageformat#47](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-messageformat/pull/47)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) presented feedback from TG1: the MessageFormat 2 parser is a novel DSL that needs experience before Stage 2, so an option is to drop it and require a data model passed to the constructor (parser cost is roughly 2 kB; a full MF2 implementation about 4 kB). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the proposal must be motivated on its own without the syntax and worried that the data model is just as unproven as the syntax; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) worried that people would invent ad-hoc serialisation (e.g. JSON) and create a competing solution; [USA](../../people/USA.md) supported dropping the syntax rather than delaying everything. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) concluded that there was not enough information to decide, asked for team positions (Mozilla, Apple, Google) next month, and the topic was carried over.

## Stable Formatting: Choose between general and specific solutions #17

- wiki: [Stable Formatting](../../proposals/stable-formatting.md)
- proposal: [proposal-stable-formatting#17](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-stable-formatting/issues/17)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked whether [Stable Formatting](../../proposals/stable-formatting.md) should pursue alternative non-Intl APIs for each misuse case or a `null` (or `zxx`) locale giving neutral, unlocalised output. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) favoured the `null` locale (helps engines embed Intl without CLDR data, and makes fixing call sites a one-line change), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) noted that output already varies by platform and that neutral output would need a defined contents, and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) pointed to root collation and untailored segmentation as existing non-locale behaviour. The sense of the discussion was interest in exploring what a `null` locale would look like; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked for a clearer statement of goals to align the group. No decision.

## Decimal integration #856

- proposal: [ecma402#856](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/856)

[JMN](../../people/JMN.md) (Decimal co-champion) introduced Decimal128 and asked whether to integrate into ECMA-402 thinly (convert to string) or thickly, and where beyond `NumberFormat` it matters. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) added `PluralRules`; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) mentioned `RelativeTimeFormat`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) advised carrying Decimal128 precision through the whole pipeline (extending the Intl mathematical value and inferring precision from the input when no options are given), and [USA](../../people/USA.md) agreed the existing structure already fits. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) suggested the spec change be kept mostly editorial. [JMN](../../people/JMN.md) accepted the thorough-integration direction; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said this needs little further TG2 time.

## Era Display: Data-driven approach #9

- proposal: [proposal-intl-eradisplay#9](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-eradisplay/issues/9)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) described a bug in the `era: "auto"` behaviour and a CLDR-suggested data-driven approach in which each calendar/era gives a date range where the era is suppressed (e.g. Gregorian roughly 1500 to 10000), instead of "hide the current era". [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) stressed readability, e.g. keeping the Buddhist era visible, and had proposed extra option values to force the era on or off. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked whether it belongs in locale data and how ranges are expressed per era. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) to update the proposal with the new information for discussion next month.

## Brief User Locale Preferences update

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) reported that the proposal had changed substantially, cutting scope toward the earlier variant-based approach (1-10 variants per locale that cannot individually identify a user). The group is at a crossroads over which entropy level is acceptable and whether to gather data, likely via CLDR. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wanted [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) and [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) kept in the loop given their motivation and privacy concerns; [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) stressed privacy as Apple's main focus.

## Editorial: Rename _dataLocaleData_ aliases to the more accurate _resolvedLocaleData_ #849

- proposal: [ecma402#849](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/849)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md)'s editorial PR renames the confusing `dataLocaleData` alias to `resolvedLocaleData`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred Anba's broader change (#853) that also renames the `[[DataLocale]]` field, and said splitting into alias renames and field renames would be acceptable. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) agreed to check the other aliases and to avoid a capital-`D` difference being the only distinction between a field and a slot name.
