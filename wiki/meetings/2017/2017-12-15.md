## Overview

The first TG2 call (no moderator is named in the notes; [DE](../../people/DE.md) gave the introduction), whose stated aim was to agree how to move ECMA-402 proposals forward through discussion among experts and to build agreement between implementations. The notes follow the numbered agenda only loosely. The main exchanges were the size cost of `Intl.Segmenter` (Mozilla's concern), the near-term Stage 3 candidates `Intl.ListFormat` and `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat`, shortcut date/time options (`dateStyle`/`timeStyle`) versus the options bag, and the meeting cadence (monthly, shortly before each TC39 meeting). Abbreviations are normalised to delegates.txt: Eemeli → [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), MS → MLS, SM → STM, CC → CCN, FB → FBN; NC (Nebojša Ćirić), JS (Jungshik Shin), JH (Jack Horton) and DIV (Doug Ilijev) are not in delegates.txt and are kept as written (the speaker "Doug" is DIV). An unlabeled "J:" line in the dateStyle discussion and a "JH (?)" attribution on the implementer-intent question are uncertain in the notes.

## Introduction and intention of this meeting

[DE](../../people/DE.md) gave a quick overview of ECMA-402: the bug tracker holds many proposed new APIs, and the goal of the meeting was to work out the process for advancing them and for building agreement between implementations. NC said Google had assembled a spreadsheet of the APIs built into Google Closure, some of which Google would like to propose to ECMA-402.

## Overview of new features in ECMA 402 after v2

- proposal: [ecma402#47](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/47)

An implementer's-intent round on `formatToParts`, `PluralRules` and `hourCycle`. JH (speaking for Chakra) considered `formatToParts` straightforward but `hourCycle` more complex, in particular how `hourTwelve: false` interacts with `-u-hc-h12`.

## Overview of advanced pending proposals and their status

- proposal: [proposal-intl-segmenter](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-segmenter)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-list-format](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-list-format)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-relative-time](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-relative-time)

`Intl.Segmenter` (listed as Stage 3 on the agenda): [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) raised Mozilla's concern about size, since Segmenter would add about 3 MB, a 100% increase over the rest of Intl; JS put the figure at 800K without the zh/ja dictionaries but with Khmer/Burmese/Lao/Thai dictionaries, and said Chrome on Android leaves out the Chinese and Japanese dictionaries. [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) noted that the Unicode standards allow customised breaking, and that extended grapheme clusters are locale-dependent. [CP](../../people/CP.md) suggested taking the rest offline.

`Intl.ListFormat` (Stage 2): [DE](../../people/DE.md) planned to ask for Stage 3 at the next plenary, saying most design questions should be settled by Stage 3. [CP](../../people/CP.md) found it strong from the editorial point of view and had no objection; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) described ~5 patterns per language, noted it appears often alongside UnitFormat, and also mentioned a NumberFormat extension aware of units.

`Intl.RelativeTimeFormat` (Stage 2): [DE](../../people/DE.md) wanted to recommend Stage 3. It includes `formatToParts`, which had no ICU support yet and would need an ICU extension; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) had implemented it for SpiderMonkey. [CP](../../people/CP.md): "hopefully we can get it in". No formal conclusion was recorded.

## Less advanced proposals

- proposal: [proposal-ecma402-datetime-style](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-ecma402-datetime-style)

`Intl.UnitFormat` (Stage 1): [DE](../../people/DE.md) noted that one option is to follow the ICU API, which is not compositional and covers only a few unit types; [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) said Shane ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md)) had a different proposal following a fluent, chainable style. `Intl.DurationFormat` appears in the agenda here (linked to ecma402#47) but was not discussed in the notes.

`dateStyle`/`timeStyle`: [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) argued that an options bag suits the constructor but not a higher-level API, where users mostly want "short"/"long"/"normal" (as in OS settings), and that these could be made locale-sensitive (for example `dateStyle: short` meaning `month: short` in one language and `2-digit` in another). A speaker noted this was part of an earlier proposal that was dropped in favour of the options bag; [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) observed users may want more settings than the standard four. [DE](../../people/DE.md) suggested `navigator.locales` could expose OS calendar preferences and that `Intl.Locale` would allow parsing and modifying a language tag. NC felt the idea mixes locales and user preferences; [SRL](../../people/SRL.md) replied that user preferences are expressed as locales (`-u` extensions).

## Future

[DE](../../people/DE.md) proposed a few calls like this before considering a face-to-face meeting. NC suggested one call per month, scheduled a week (or a few days) before each TC39 meeting (every three months); Microsoft and Mozilla agreed. [CP](../../people/CP.md) noted only one more TC39 meeting remained before the next edition was cut, so focus should be on proposals that could reach Stage 4 (and maybe Stage 3). DIV asked for a less dense agenda and for feedback to be collected before the meeting. The notes record agreement from the Microsoft and Mozilla attendees on a monthly cadence, with no formal conclusion line.
