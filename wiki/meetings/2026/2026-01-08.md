## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md). TG2 agreed to recommend to CLDR that the `iso8601` calendar keep `-` and `:` separators in all locales and default to the `h23` hour cycle, approved the `tols` numbering system (#1035) for TG1, and approved the Intl Era and MonthCode PR that closes the calendar list and drops `islamic-rgsa` (#99, pending [HJS](../../people/HJS.md)'s review). Two Intl Era and MonthCode issues (Meiji start and `MonthDay` reference years) were discussed with proposed resolutions but no formal conclusion. YSK in the notes is read as [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md).

## iso8601 as DateTimeFormat calendar #1036

- proposal: [ecma402#1036](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/1036)

Following last month, [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) clarified that the CLDR problem is locale-dependent separators and spelled-out forms with `iso8601` (e.g. `ja` with `dateStyle: "long"`), which CLDR 48 did not ship. [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) want the shipped short-form behaviour kept (year-month-day with `-`, time with `:`, `h23` as in Firefox and Safari but not Chrome), and care little about the long forms or the fractional-second separator. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) also asked whether ECMA should be a Unicode member; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) saw no concrete benefit beyond acting as liaison. The group preferred aligning with CLDR before writing any 402 spec text, and StableFormatting is a separate effort. The issue stays open to decide on spec text later. **Conclusion: TG2 will recommend to CLDR to keep `-` and `:` as the date and time separators in all locales when using `u-ca-iso8601`, and to default to the `h23` hour cycle unless another hour cycle is explicitly requested.** [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [HJS](../../people/HJS.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) gave +1.

## Intl Energy Units: Unit Choices

- proposal: [ecma402#739](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/739)

The proposal covers `watt`, `kilowatt` and `kilowatt-hour`; TG1 advanced it to Stage 1 without joules, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) is co-champion (mentoring invited expert Kaleb). Open questions were `watt-hour` (not in CLDR), `megawatt` and friends, and calories. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) judged joules unmotivated by web content and `watt-hour` weak; [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) preferred framing it as consumer-facing electricity rather than energy in general; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) worried about a slippery slope unless the scope is limited to EV and building/power use. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed asking CLDR to add unit preferences (e.g. vehicle efficiency) and feeding the results back, delaying a Stage 2 request by a couple of months. No formal decision; the agreed next step is the CLDR unit preference work.

## Be explicit about the Gregorian to Meiji switchover month and day in the Japanese calendar #86

- proposal: [proposal-intl-era-monthcode#86](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-era-monthcode/issues/86)

[HJS](../../people/HJS.md) wants all implementers and CLDR to agree on one approach and prefers starting the Meiji era at 6 Meiji (1873), since earlier Meiji dates were lunisolar and reading them as Gregorian causes mistakes. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) agreed that a Meiji 1-5 date would resolve to the Gregorian date. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s proposed resolution: dates up to 1872-12-31 use Gregorian eras, dates from 1873-01-01 use the solar Japanese eras defined by the government of Japan, and those eras are defined proleptically to align with Gregorian outside their range. [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) agreed with the substance but was unsure of the last sentence's wording. No formal conclusion was recorded.

## Stage 2.7 review feedback: Provide guidance on calculating a reference year for MonthDay when not provided. #60

- proposal: [proposal-intl-era-monthcode#60](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-era-monthcode/issues/60)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) introduced the options for `MonthDay` reference years. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (worried about forward compatibility when the year range is extended) and [HJS](../../people/HJS.md) favoured Option B over hard-coded years or engine freedom, and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) came round to it because it keeps serialization web-compatible. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) would always constrain a leap month to non-leap, and `toPlainMonthDay` should constrain by default; [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) noted a Temporal v2 issue to add an option for that. For a clean Stage 3 the PR only needs to be written by the agenda deadline, without returning to TG2.
