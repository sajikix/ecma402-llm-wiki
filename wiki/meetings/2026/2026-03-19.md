## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (co-moderator). Status updates were skipped for lack of time and no upcoming plenary. The meeting was mostly design discussion with no formal conclusions, apart from [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s proposed (not agreed) conclusion on [eraDisplay](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md) cutoffs. A disagreement over what TG1 had decided for [Intl Keep Trailing Zeros](../../proposals/intl-keep-trailing-zeros.md) issue #15 was deferred to the numerics call, the [Unit Protocol](../../proposals/intl-unit-protocol.md) fallback logic for non-protocol objects (#5) was debated but left open, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) opened a discussion on sequence unit formatting (#398) and on criteria for accepting new unit usages (#1047). For Intl [EraDisplay](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md), the group discussed a year-style option and its name (`algebraic` and `linear` had no objections) and rejected using the current date to choose between two- and four-digit years.

## Precision when rendering very small values as zero #15

- wiki: [Intl Keep Trailing Zeros](../../proposals/intl-keep-trailing-zeros.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-keep-trailing-zeros#15](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-keep-trailing-zeros/issues/15)

The question was how values that round to zero (e.g. `"0.0001"` with `maximumFractionDigits: 3`) should render. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) read TG1's notes ("resolved as requested") as settling on `0.000` (only relevant with `notation: "scientific"` or `"engineering"`), whereas [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) understood TG1 as asking for further TG2 discussion and prefers treating such zeros as leading zeros, formatted as a single zero since no significant digits remain. The two sides disagreed on whether significant or fraction digits are the right lens and on what TG1 concluded. No decision was made; the discussion was pointed to the numerics call, and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) to restate their position on the issue.

## Fallback logic for existing, non-protocol implementing, object parameters #5

- wiki: [Intl Unit Protocol](../../proposals/intl-unit-protocol.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-unit-protocol#5](https://github.com/sffc/proposal-intl-unit-protocol/issues/5)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) argued the protocol is applied too eagerly, since existing objects would be routed to a `.value` accessor that may not exist, and proposed triggering it only for objects with `value` (and `unit`). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) listed two sub-options for the check: test the prototype chain for `unit` like the `in` operator, or read `unit` and require it to be non-`undefined` (`null` allowed); both fall back to `toPrimitive` if the shape is absent. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred only checking that the properties exist, without validating types, so custom numeric types still work; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) cautioned that a `hasProperty` check followed by a `Get` would traverse the prototype chain twice, which would need a new abstract operation returning both existence and value. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) had a mild preference for the presence check because an `Amount` without a unit has `undefined` rather than `null`. No decision; the discussion continues in April.

## Sequence unit formatting #398

- proposal: [ecma402#398](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/398)

[WH](../../people/WH.md) asked in TG1 that sequence unit formatting be designed before [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md) reaches Stage 2, so [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed splitting it into its own proposal and possibly requesting Stage 2 directly. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) suggested supporting the CLDR `-and-` infix (e.g. `foot-and-inch`); the open design question is which unit the input value is indexed to: CLDR uses the largest unit, while [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) also described indexing by the smallest unit so that operations stay in clean integers. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) said this should be at least Stage 1, wants to study CLDR design docs and other libraries first, and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) asked why CLDR chose the largest unit, giving degrees/arcminutes/arcseconds as an example. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) volunteered to research CLDR's reasoning; nothing was decided.

## Quantitative criteria for accepting more unit usages #1047

- proposal: [ecma402#1047](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/1047)

Background: ECMA-402 has 45 units chosen mostly by TG2 judgment in 2019, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) presented [Intl Energy Units](../../proposals/intl-energy-units.md) as a new domain-motivated addition and asked whether explicit (e.g. quantitative) criteria are needed. JSC asked whether domains such as `length-road` or `mass-person` may overlap, how new domains (health, weather) would be judged, whether the process is proactive or ad hoc, and whether they would be one proposal or several. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) replied that a usage implies its subsets, that the committee has preferred individually motivated proposals backed by developer input, and that adding units could eventually be a lightweight normative PR like numbering systems; [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) and JSC noted that domain experts are needed, with JSC interested in helping on health units. No decision; the group is continuing with individually motivated proposals.

## Year precision #10

- wiki: [eraDisplay option for Intl.DateTimeFormat](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-eradisplay#10](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-eradisplay/issues/10)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed extending Intl [EraDisplay](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md) beyond era-only options to year styles like ICU4X's `YearStyle` (`Auto`, `Full`, `WithEra`, `NoEra`), including whether to add a numeric-style option matching Temporal's `year` (negative years for BCE) as [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) requested, for astronomers. Since "numeric" is already overloaded, the group compared names (`algebraic`, `linear`, `numeric`, `monotonic`, `arithmetic`, `extended`); [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) objected to `extended` (claimed by ISO 8601) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) all had no objection to `algebraic` or `linear`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted the earlier requirement for an implementation is now met via ICU4X. No final name was chosen.

## Data-driven approach #9

- wiki: [eraDisplay option for Intl.DateTimeFormat](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-eradisplay#9](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-eradisplay/issues/9)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) favoured deciding when to show era or century from locale data rather than hard-coding it: Japanese always shows the era, and ICU4X models this with a `YearAmbiguity` enum. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) preferred hard cutoffs over anything depending on the current clock, and noted that fixing cutoffs in ECMAScript would carry an expectation they never change. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s proposed conclusion was to not use the current date, and instead to take the cutoffs from locale data, which may change with browser versions (cf. the locale data synced to browser version PR); [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) suggested constants tied to the CLDR version. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked who will update the proposal, which has not changed in six years, and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) showed interest. The notes record this as a proposed conclusion rather than a recorded agreement.
