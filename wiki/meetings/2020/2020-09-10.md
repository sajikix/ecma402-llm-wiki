## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). TG2 reached consensus for `Intl.DisplayNames` to go to Stage 4 ([FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to add it to the TC39 agenda). The main design discussion was how `Intl.DurationFormat` should treat [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)'s new signed durations (issue #29): the sketch that emerged was to keep `Intl.DurationFormat` for unsigned durations and take relative/signed formatting to `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat` separately. The group also discussed the naming of the clock-like style (#16), error handling for the dotted format (#33), the `formatRange` path to Stage 4, and two ecma402 PRs (#493 GetOption reform, #500 OrdinaryHasInstance), the latter held back. Abbreviations are as in the notes (no ad-hoc ones); every speaker is in the attendee list.

## MessageFormat Working Group

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md) reported the first task force meeting, which looked at selectors and sub-selectors; the main group will formalise which to choose from a short list. The group is improving its process so each meeting has a clean proposal, and is planning the talk at the Unicode Conference.

## Intl.DisplayNames toward Stage 4

- proposal: [proposal-intl-displaynames](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-displaynames)

The open question was canonicalization ([#82](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-displaynames/issues/82) script, [#81](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-displaynames/issues/81) region): Anba wants language, script and region canonicalized, whereas [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) would canonicalize only language, since UTS 35 canonicalizes scripts only in the context of a locale and the 402 spec lets a code return any string. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) is handling enhancement requests in Intl.DisplayNames V2. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) saw the #81 problems as mostly theoretical and not blocking; feedback can be handled in later revisions. **Conclusion: Stage 4 consensus. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) will add it to the TC39 agenda.**

## Negative durations in Intl.DurationFormat #29

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#29](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/29)

[USA](../../people/USA.md) explained that [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) durations are now always signed (mixed signs are invalid) and asked whether the proposal should instead extend `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat`. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) argued that a duration has no reference point and a negative duration should not exist in the data model, while [USA](../../people/USA.md), [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) and [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) pointed to real use (subtracting dates, "1 day ago"); [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) cited the [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) cookbook motivation for adding the sign. [USA](../../people/USA.md) listed three styles: relative to now, relative to an arbitrary point, and unsigned. The discussion converged on splitting the work: continue `Intl.DurationFormat` for unsigned durations, and work on `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat` extensions (compound units, later arbitrary reference points) separately. [USA](../../people/USA.md) noted `NumberFormat` lacks mixed-unit support. Whether `Temporal.Duration.prototype.toLocaleString` should use `DurationFormat` (ignoring or throwing on sign), as [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) favoured for discoverability, or be removed, as [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) suggested, stayed open. **Conclusion: no formal decision; [USA](../../people/USA.md) will bring the thoughts back to the champions group.**

## Handling wrong arguments for dotted format #33

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#33](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/33)

[YMD](../../people/YMD.md) introduced the problem; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) framed it as fall back or throw, and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) and [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) leaned towards throwing, noting a duration's fields are not known until it is formatted. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) questioned the need for the dotted format ([YMD](../../people/YMD.md): video games) and how the largest/smallest fields are picked, and asked for code samples on the issue. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted the Stage 2 proposal uses an array instead of `largestUnit`/`smallestUnit`.

## "dotted" versus "numeric" #16

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#16](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/16)

A naming brainstorm for the clock-like style that renders "4:03": [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) suggested `"symbol"`, `"delimiter"`, `"delimited"`, `"concise"` and `"digitalClock"`; [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) `"delimited"` or `"concise"`; [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) `"digital"`; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred `"digital"` as self-describing but also floated `"clock"` and `"symbolic"`, wanting a name that could also cover coordinates and heights. [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) was not a fan of `"digital"`, and [USA](../../people/USA.md) liked `"concise"`. **Conclusion: none recorded; the names remain open.**

## Normative: Use OrdinaryHasInstance in normative optional steps #500

- proposal: [ecma402#500](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/500)

[JSW](../../people/JSW.md) said the change simplifies semantics by avoiding symbol lookups, and could break only people who deliberately rely on `hasInstance` (and some tests). [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) worried about breakage. **Conclusion: [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) held the PR back to consider the consequences, to be brought to TC39 in November with no rush.**
