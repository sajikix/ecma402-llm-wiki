## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md). The meeting was dominated by status updates a week before the February plenary: DateTimeFormat gap behaviour went to CLDR and came back favourable, `dateStyle`/`timeStyle` and Locale prepared for Stage 4, `Intl.Segmenter` was held back by size and random-access concerns, and [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) (Stage 0, ahead of its Stage 1 request) was debated at length without a conclusion. [FBN](../../people/FBN.md) speaks but is not in the attendee list. `BK` is BKL (Brian Kardell).

## MessageFormat Working Group

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) reported two presentations, a discussion of HTML syntax, and a plan to split the work into many smaller issues.

## Normative: Add calendar and numberingSystem options (#175)

- proposal: [ecma402#175](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/175)

[CLA](../../people/CLA.md) said test262 coverage is now complete and, with SpiderMonkey implementing, every box is ticked to ask for merge. [ABL](../../people/ABL.md) raised a spec issue: lowercasing of the values is missing (also for `DateTimeFormat` and calendar), observable through `resolvedOptions`. [DE](../../people/DE.md) explained the spec previously relied on the lowercasing in UTS 35 and will now define ASCII case mapping explicitly. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted the one use-case concern had been resolved with its raiser.

## Normative: Add dayPeriod option #346

- proposal: [ecma402#346](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/346)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) reported that CLDR liked the idea of handling the gaps in `DateTimeFormat` options (issue #394 from the previous meeting) and chose approach #3, filling the gaps, so doing nothing in ECMA-402 means this gets solved in CLDR. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) cautioned that someone will ask how to _not_ add months, and that the decision should consider both `DateTimeFormat` and the new duration API since they differ. [ABL](../../people/ABL.md) wanted to wait for the next ICU release rather than integrate individual commits, since ICU does not yet give the expected output; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) would follow up with [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) on V8.

## Intl.Segmenter (Stage 2)

- proposal: [proposal-intl-segmenter](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-segmenter)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) described the redesign: two classes split into three, with `Intl.Segmenter` holding locale and options and `.segments(string)` returning an iterable `Segments` object with `.contains(i)` for stateless random access. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) was uncomfortable with the data size increase and wanted time to evaluate low-data environments; MGR called it a spec concern too, since reliance on behaviour would de facto mandate ICU, and questioned the need for random access (a flawed approach in most cases). [DE](../../people/DE.md) said random access was added on request, for parity with `v8BreakIterator` and ICU, and that line-break segmentation had been removed. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said Google wants it advanced; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) agreed to wait a month. **Outcome: [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) will give a Stage 2 update at the plenary instead of asking for advancement.**

## Intl.DurationFormat (Stage 0)

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format](https://github.com/younies/proposal-intl-duration-format)

[YMD](../../people/YMD.md) (the champion; up for Stage 1 the next week) wanted to add "rounding" and "hide" options, such as dropping the zero minutes from "1 hour 0 minutes 30 seconds". [CCN](../../people/CCN.md) objected that hiding intermediate units is wrong (for example "1 day and 30 seconds"), though [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) noted "one year two days" does make sense; [CCN](../../people/CCN.md) also described showing only the two most significant units. [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) wanted an "easy mode" with sensible defaults, and [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) suggested a few named modes such as flight duration or timer. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) advised that ECMA-402 aims to be correct by default, so hiding a middle value should be visibly special; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) suggested per-unit values like `auto`/`if-not-zero`/`round`. On rounding, [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) set the principle: "I think we should try to make the math all on the Temporal side, and [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) should optionally filter fields and display them, but no math." [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) would have a Temporal duration method round to a smallest field. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) closed by saying the timer case must be supported to justify the API. No conclusion was recorded.
