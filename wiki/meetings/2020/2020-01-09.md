## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md). The main business was a set of open design questions: whether ECMA-402 should list supported calendars (no way forward chosen), how to handle discontiguous fields in `Intl.DateTimeFormat` options (taken to CLDR), and what to do when `dateStyle`/`timeStyle` are combined with other options (consensus to throw a `RangeError`). The group also had a first design discussion of [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md), which ended with "Stage 1 consensus" and a plan for [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) to present at the February plenary. Ad-hoc abbreviations in the notes were normalised: `LB` (as in "LB: It connects more to datetime format...") is [LEO](../../people/LEO.md), `LH` is LHO, and `SFL` is SRL.

## MessageFormat Working Group

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md) reported two meetings with maintainers of i18next, Fluent and FBT presenting their message format requirements, and plans to collect requirements as GitHub issues so the WG can filter them before building MessageFormat. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted the meetings have a good group.

## Issue: Make List of Supported Calendars

- proposal: [ecma402#395](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/395)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued that with Temporal a calendar must be usable for date math, so any calendar supported for formatting should also be supported for math, and that a minimum list like the one for numbering systems would give programmers a firm expectation (for example Hebrew calendar math). SRL objected that ECMA-402 should not maintain its own repository of locale data, and that implementations will support the calendars preferred in the locations they serve. LHO said a mandatory calendar list would be hard for a polyfill (data size plus logic). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) floated the weaker rule "any calendar that is supported for formatting must be supported for math", with a preferred list possibly added later. No direction was chosen; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed to "kick the can down the road".

## Decide behavior with gaps in Intl.DateTimeFormat options (replacement pattern)

- proposal: [ecma402#394](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/394)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) listed four options for option combinations that do not make sense (such as year plus day without month): garbage in garbage out; throw; implicitly fill the gaps; or get linguistic data from CLDR. [LEO](../../people/LEO.md) preferred the first, saying filling is normalisation that belongs in a library and would be permanent once chosen; [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) agreed. [DE](../../people/DE.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted the current spec (the basic format matcher) already allows implementations to fill in the month, and [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) had thought this was already well specified. [CLA](../../people/CLA.md) worried that two compliant implementations could produce different output. SRL suggested asking CLDR for linguistic input, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) agreed to put it on the CLDR agenda and report back; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the new `fractionalSecondDigits`/`dayPeriod`-style options make garbage combinations more likely.

## Issue: Consider error checking for using dateStyle/timeStyle together with other options

- proposal: [proposal-intl-datetime-style#2](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-datetime-style/issues/2)

Currently `dateStyle`/`timeStyle` silently win over other options. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued that throwing now would leave room to add a feature later (for example, modifying the style by turning off the year) without a web-compat problem, and proposed a `RangeError`. [DE](../../people/DE.md) supported throwing; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) explained the old model (fields, best-skeleton matching) versus the new one (locale styles) and said the combination is simply unspecified. VYG asked for consistency with the discontiguous-fields question; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) answered that the older API cannot start throwing without breaking the web. [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) said throwing is reasonable for SpiderMonkey. **Conclusion: Consensus (throw); [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) will write the patch and ask [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) for review.**

## Intl.DurationFormat

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [ecma402#47](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/47)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md)'s motivation is `toLocaleString` support for Temporal's duration type. The question was where it should live: `DateTimeFormat`, `NumberFormat` (hours and minutes are units, and ICU's MeasureFormat is NumberFormat-like and has a "numeric form"), or a new class. [DE](../../people/DE.md) initially assumed overloading `DateTimeFormat`; [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) also expected it there; [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) said it belongs in `DateTimeFormat` or its own class; [LEO](../../people/LEO.md) (as `LB`) preferred a new class for learnability; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) wanted a standalone class closest to `RelativeTimeFormat`, avoiding strange option bags and easier to deprecate once folded into `NumberFormat`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said Temporal Stage 3 was targeted for the March/April plenary and this should be at least Stage 2 by then. **Decision: Stage 1 consensus; [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) will compile pros and cons of the options, bring them to the next meeting and present Stage 1 in February.**
