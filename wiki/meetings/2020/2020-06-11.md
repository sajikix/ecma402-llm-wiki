## Overview

Moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md). [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) gave the first TG2 presentation of [Smart Unit Preferences](../../proposals/smart-unit-preferences.md), where [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) and [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) pressed for use cases and building blocks before an end-to-end API. TG2 supported Stage 4 for `Intl.ListFormat` and for `dateStyle`/`timeStyle` (the latter pending editorial issues), discussed user preferences on the web platform at length (a possible champion group of [ZB](../../people/ZB.md), [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), with W3C, PING and client hints as venues), and reached general agreement to support the `-u-rg` region override subtag. The meeting ran over time and moved Segmenter and meeting format changes to the next meeting.

## MessageFormat Working Group

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md) listed the next-meeting agenda: why MessageFormat needs a successor, and design principles. [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) said the roadmap was set, the WG's scope, goals and non-goals were defined at the last meeting, and "real" work was starting.

## Smart Unit Preferences

- wiki: [Smart Unit Preferences in Intl.NumberFormat](../../proposals/smart-unit-preferences.md)
- proposal: [proposal-smart-unit-preferences](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-smart-unit-preferences)

[YMD](../../people/YMD.md) introduced locale-aware unit conversion and formatting. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) first asked whether an ECMA-402 issue existed ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md): #277), and was concerned that the proposal reached TC39 before TG2; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) suggested exposing the preferred unit (building blocks) and letting libraries do the conversion, wary of feature creep and noting the lack of end-to-end prior art and user demand. [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) agreed that API level should follow users and use cases, and asked how many `usage` values exist ([YMD](../../people/YMD.md): about 10 in CLDR). [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) saw conversion and formatting as third-party library territory. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) replied that the shape is open, both approaches have merit, and asked [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) and [HUG](../../people/HUG.md) to research how this is solved in JS; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) also said the proposal meets the Stage 1 bar of a worthwhile area, and cited CLDR inflection work as another benefit. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) asked for a better name than "smart". No conclusion was recorded.

## ListFormat

- proposal: [ecma402#33](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/33)

[ZB](../../people/ZB.md) said the proposal was ready for Stage 4 and, with no objection, wanted to open the PR against ECMA-402; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) confirmed all requirements were met (shipped in Chrome, behind a flag in Firefox). [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) pointed to a V2 issue ([proposal-intl-list-format#51](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-list-format/issues/51)). **Conclusion: Stage 4! [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) will work on the PR.**

## short/medium/long/full style for Intl.DateTimeFormat

- proposal: [ecma402#108](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/108)

[ZB](../../people/ZB.md) reported one remaining non-editorial issue ([proposal-intl-datetime-style#49](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-datetime-style/issues/49)) on what happens when a style is combined with other fields, and the choice among options. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) leaned to option 1 or 3 and hoped CLDR resolution would later allow dropping the TypeError for inconsistent bags; [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) preferred option 1 because `dateStyle`/`timeStyle` are orthogonal to the other fields, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) agreed. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) raised the new Stage 4 issue #46 and test coverage for the throwing behaviour, which V8 did not yet implement; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) would add the coverage request. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) noted this is the last proposal championed from the batch picked up in 2015. **Conclusion: Stage 4, pending final resolution on the open editorial issues.**

## Intl.Locale minimize/maximize extension keys

- proposal: [ecma402#409](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/409)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) presented the user-preferences direction (issue #6 and a sketch in #409, related to #416 from the February onsite): `navigator.locales` as a set of Unicode keywords with a "likely calendar" lookup falling back to system settings, plus contact with the client-hints people and a meeting with privacy experts on Monday. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) distinguished "first day of week for this locale" from "what the user requested", and noted platforms differ on whether overrides apply to all locales or only the primary. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), with agreement from [ZB](../../people/ZB.md), said W3C/WHATWG, not TC39, is the right home and ECMA-402 should not speak about user preferences; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) replied the CLDR fallback logic is in TG2's expertise and a holistic approach is needed. [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) suggested the W3C Privacy Interest Group. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) floated an early champion group of [ZB](../../people/ZB.md), [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), and [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) and [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) confirmed interest; a new discussion group for user preferences on the web platform was opened. No formal conclusion beyond that.

## Region Override Support

- proposal: [ecma402#370](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/370)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed supporting the `rg` subtag (for example `en-US-u-rg-RO` for someone using US English with Romanian preferences). [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) cited Firefox users wanting `en-US` with European preferences, and [USA](../../people/USA.md) noted `en-DK`-style locales use GB spellings. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked for the definition of `rg` ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md): specified in UTS 35). [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) did not want ICU support to drive what ECMA-402 includes, but accepted that implementations depend on it. **Outcome: general agreement to support the `rg` keyword (no formal conclusion line).**

## Proposal and PR Progress Tracking

- proposal: [Proposal and PR Progress Tracking](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/wiki/Proposal-and-PR-Progress-Tracking)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) reviewed unmerged PRs and noted that `RelativeTimeFormat` lacks `length`, then asked the group to go for consistency for now (agreements recorded as `<agreements>`). For Segmenter, [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the TG2 members want it advanced but the private-fields issue must be resolved by TC39; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) asked whether the V8 break iterator will be removed, and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) gave usage numbers (0.65% of pages use a break iterator, 0.4% for words, 0.07% for line breaks) to support deprecation once Segmenter ships without line breaking. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) said the current spec has only a weird collection of properties, fixed by a PR. Formal discussion was deferred to the next meeting, along with changing the meeting format.
