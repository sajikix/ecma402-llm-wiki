## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). The file's own title reads "December 12, 2020 Meeting", but the filename and meeting date used here are 2020-12-04. The long discussion was ECMA-402's API surface and scope guidelines (PR #517), where payload size and implementability were debated. TG2 then gave Stage 1 consensus to the `eraDisplay` option for `Intl.DateTimeFormat` and to `Intl.LocaleMatcher`, and approved Stage 2 for [Intl Locale Info](../../proposals/intl-locale-info.md) after reshaping its defaults and raising doubts about the measurement system model. Extend TimeZoneName was introduced and its Stage 2 prerequisites outlined. `formatRange` remained on track for a January Stage 4 request.

## formatRange for Stage 4

- proposal: [proposal-intl-DateTimeFormat-formatRange](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-DateTimeFormat-formatRange/)

[FBN](../../people/FBN.md): still planning January; open issues remain and test262 PRs were sent; waiting for an LGTM from `anba` on #25 ([JSW](../../people/JSW.md) pinged `anba` and the Bugzilla bug), and for `fractionalSecondDigits` to be submitted. ## API surface and scope guidelines #442

- proposal: [ecma402#517](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/517)

[ZB](../../people/ZB.md) argued that prior art is a double-edged signal and that a separation between algorithms and data size should be a condition. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) worried the wide-adoption requirement would exclude MessageFormat v2 ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md): the "best i18n practice" judgement suffices). MLS (Apple) raised startup-time concerns about payload growth for new features, versus growth in existing political data such as IANA time zones ([LHO](../../people/LHO.md)); [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [LHO](../../people/LHO.md) noted the spec allows implementations with minimal data and is best-effort, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) added that JSC uses OS data. MLS asked that the spec not require a particular backing library; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) noted a browser could download data on demand and still conform; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) wanted the impact on new market entrants balanced; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said 20 KiB is no longer a huge change. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed checking in PR #517 as Stage 2 entrance requirements, treating implementation issues such as locale data as Stage 3 criteria (as was done for NumberFormat v2 with the quantity of measurement units), and discussing specifics with TG1. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md): SGTM.

## eraDisplay option for Intl.DateTimeFormat for Stage 1

- wiki: [eraDisplay option for Intl.DateTimeFormat](../../proposals/intl-eradisplay.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-eradisplay](https://github.com/Louis-Aime/proposal-intl-eradisplay)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1CABEQP_U-vCUxGKXbJmaZKvJZHEdFZZtAHGAOnRbrCY/edit)

[LAF](../../people/LAF.md) presented. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) saw value in `"also"` and `"always"` but not `"never"`; [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) said the era can be implied for very ancient Gregorian dates, and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) gave a Japanese-era use case; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred keeping `"never"` for spec consistency and noted a formatter could be initialised both ways in the constructor. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) also wanted to display the era by itself, and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) observed that time zone and era are special because requesting them also renders the rest of the data. [CCN](../../people/CCN.md), [ZB](../../people/ZB.md), [JSW](../../people/JSW.md), [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) gave +1. **Conclusion: approved for Stage 1.**

## Intl.LocaleMatcher for Stage 1

- proposal: [proposal-intl-localematcher](https://github.com/longlho/proposal-intl-localematcher)

[LHO](../../people/LHO.md) presented exposing the internal locale negotiation so message, date and number formatting stay coherent (e.g. for loading translations). [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked where the default locale comes from; [LHO](../../people/LHO.md) said it is the site's choice and a non-supported request currently falls back to it. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked about a locale-distance function instead ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md): a good idea; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) linked a related comment). [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) raised Unicode extensions and nested negotiation, said the result should be a list rather than a string, and wanted close coordination with MessageFormat v2, while [LHO](../../people/LHO.md) did not want to couple them strongly (images, voice and video are also in play). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) felt the open questions can be settled in Stage 1. [CCN](../../people/CCN.md), [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) gave +1. **Conclusion: Stage 1 approved.**

## Intl Locale Info

- wiki: [Intl Locale Info](../../proposals/intl-locale-info.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-locale-info](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-locale-info)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed moving `measurementSystem` from `.unitInfo` to `.defaults` and making everything in `.defaults` an ordered list of preferences; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) agreed and suggested a name like "preferred", to which [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) replied with `.prefs` (so `locale.prefs.calendar[0]`). On whether to separate locale data from resolved preferences, [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) preferred one getter (remove the extension keywords to get pure locale data). [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) asked whether some places mix metric and imperial per unit, beyond US/metric/UK; [USA](../../people/USA.md) agreed, noting it is "way more common in the global south", and said more investigation is needed. The notes link the [Smart Unit Preferences](../../proposals/smart-unit-preferences.md) proposal. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [JSW](../../people/JSW.md), [CCN](../../people/CCN.md) and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) gave +1. **Conclusion: Stage 2 approved.**

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) explained the proposal. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) supported and asked about fallback (use `long` when `longLocation` is unavailable); [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) thought that belongs in the spec, [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) asked whether it could be normative since it cannot be tested, and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) thought it cannot be normative. No stage decision was recorded at this meeting.
