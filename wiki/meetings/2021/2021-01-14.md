## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). A stage-heavy meeting: TG2 supported `formatRange` for Stage 4, gave consensus for Stage 2 on [Intl Locale Info](../../proposals/intl-locale-info.md), and gave consensus on Stage 1 for Extend TimeZoneName. After a long debate it narrowed `Intl.DisplayNames` V2 to `calendar` and `unit` for Stage 2, leaving date and time names to Temporal. It also settled the `DurationFormat` clock-style name (`digital`), recorded tentative `DurationFormat` decisions, and approved a duplicate-variant normative PR (#429). [YSV](../../people/YSV.md) spoke on [Intl Locale Info](../../proposals/intl-locale-info.md) but is not in the attendee list. [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) delivered the MessageFormat WG update.

## Liaison Updates: MessageFormat Working Group

[RCA](../../people/RCA.md): since the last meeting the group closed some open points, including consensus on external selectors, and made internal progress on dynamic references. Current work is the data model: reviewing and normalising four proposals and choosing a schema definition language, prototyped in TypeScript. A 2021 roadmap is planned before the next monthly meeting.

## Getting month and weekday names is easier with Temporal #4

- proposal: [intl-displaynames-v2#4](https://github.com/tc39/intl-displaynames-v2/issues/4)

[JGT](../../people/JGT.md) asked what advantage `DisplayNames` offers over doing this already, and noted Hebrew month names depend on the year. [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) argued for ergonomics and low cost since the data already ships; [RCA](../../people/RCA.md) described month-name use cases in applications (proposal-intl-displaynames#46). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said a proper API would have to handle leap years, and that the problems are largely limited to months, not weekdays; [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said no assumptions about Temporal should be made before Stage 3; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked whether to wait for Temporal; [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) asked about eras ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md) saw the point: era names matter for Japanese). [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) was sceptical about `numberingSystem`, argued that dropping month implies dropping weekday, and [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) agreed to design for use cases. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) proposed removing all date/time features and handing them to Temporal ([EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) +1); [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) wanted to keep time zones (the only way to format them) and weekdays ([PFC](../../people/PFC.md): awkward from `formatToParts`). [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) then asked about going to Stage 2 with just unit and calendar; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued both merit it (calendar display names are not exposed today and are useful for calendar apps); [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [USA](../../people/USA.md) and [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) gave +1, [USA](../../people/USA.md) noting date/time questions can be revisited in a post-Temporal world; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) asked why calendar is needed. **Conclusion: consensus to go to Stage 2 with only `calendar` and `unit`.**

## Normative: Do not allow duplicate variants within the `tlang` component of a `transformed_extensions` #429

- proposal: [ecma402#429](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/429)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the change already ships in Firefox and Chrome but consensus was never formally recorded. [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) pitched the PR (mostly editorial changes required to make the normative change; see test262#2858); [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) asked about merging and MDN, and thought MDN is probably not needed. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) gave +1. **Conclusion: consensus recorded.**

## Normative: Use OrdinaryHasInstance in normative optional steps #500

- proposal: [ecma402#500](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/500)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) asked about concerns. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) was not opposed but doubted the change justifies the risk; [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) judged the risk low (only observable via pre-ES6-style constructors). [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) said the benefit is less work per operation, while stating a general disfavour of normative changes; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked whether to tell the PR's author that it would not be accepted without a concrete performance improvement. [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) said it is about simplicity and hygiene ("why not?"). **Conclusion: consensus** (the notes' final rationale sentence is cut off).

## Add approximately pattern #10

- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#10](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/10)

[JSW](../../people/JSW.md) found it vaguely useful; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) suggested a separate option rather than a sign display option. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) framed the question as allowing implementations to collapse ranges when both sides' digits are equal and then add an approximately symbol. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) asked about hiding the symbol when the numbers are equal before rounding; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said behaviour should stay the same and developers can check `a === b` before calling `formatRange`. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) gave +1. No formal conclusion line is recorded.

## Proposal for Intl.DurationFormat
