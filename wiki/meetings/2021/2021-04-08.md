## Overview

Co-moderated by [USA](../../people/USA.md), with [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) also chairing the discussion. A stage-heavy meeting: TG2 gave consensus for Intl Locale Info to go to Stage 3 (with the shorter-list return option and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md)'s +1 contingent on wording changes), `Intl.DisplayNames` v2 to Stage 3, and Extend TimeZoneName to Stage 2 (options renamed to `shortOffset`/`longOffset`), and agreed the last two NumberFormat V3 issues (rounding options, rounding modes). The Intl Enumeration privacy evaluation was closed. For `Intl.DurationFormat`, the group settled that `smallestUnit`/`largestUnit` stay constructor options and agreed to recommend that TG1 reconsider plural unit names in Temporal. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and CCN speak but are not in the attendee list ([RGN](../../people/RGN.md) is listed twice); a few speakers are recorded as `???`, and "FYI" in the notes appears to be a typo for [FYT](../../people/FYT.md).

## Intl Locale for Stage 3: Spec Review

- proposal: [proposal-intl-locale-info](https://tc39.es/proposal-intl-locale-info/)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) presented two spec approaches for the locale getters (whether a locale with an explicit `-u-` extension such as `-u-ca-japanese` returns only that value, restricting, or the full preference list, extending). [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred Approach 2 and noted the getters need locale data (relevant for ICU4X); [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) preferred restriction since an explicit preference should be reflected, while [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) held the opposite, weakly held view because there is no way to reject a calendar. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) described two use cases (calendar negotiation, and multi-calendar regions) and wanted the first element to be the best guess of user preference. MCM and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) said WebKit and Firefox will not expose user preferences through this API, to avoid fingerprinting, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said OS data should not be reachable through ECMA-402. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred the shorter, single-element return, since the longer list is easy to recover by dropping the extension keyword. **Conclusion: propose the shorter list but document that applications can change that; no objections. Consensus for Intl Locale Info for Stage 3 from [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [USA](../../people/USA.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) (contingent on the discussed changes), [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md).**

## Intl.DisplayNames v2 for Stage 3

- proposal: [intl-displaynames-v2](https://github.com/tc39/intl-displaynames-v2)
- slide: [slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1_BR2bq6gi_i9QjDDluv683cuO2AXNwZl-3hXC4gLl3M/edit)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) summarised changes since the January Stage 2 discussion: time zones removed (calendar and unit kept), a date/time field type added, and a `dialect` option for `type: "language"` returning `dialectName` or `standardName` (e.g. "American English" vs "Australian English"; requested by IBM among others). [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) solicited better names for the fields and enum values and will fill in calendar names in the README; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) described the approved options as date field names, calendar names, unit names and dialect handling, already a fairly thin proposal. [USA](../../people/USA.md) volunteered as a Stage 3 reviewer besides [SFC](../../people/SFC.md); [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) noted web apps are full of display names and supported it. **Conclusion: no objections; TG2 supports Stage 3 with the pared-down specification (support from [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md)).**

## Extend TimeZoneName Option Proposal for Stage 2

- proposal: [proposal-intl-extend-timezonename](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-extend-timezonename/)
- slide: [slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1eYuygaDvErbftDjg4S6zCYqhaxpEiCyFDDdpjmPaMyo/)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md) presented the proposal against the Stage 2 entrance criteria (prior art such as "ET" on news sites, expense in userland due to locale data dependence, broad appeal) plus payload data showing the size is small. The only open issue, requests for more ISO-8601 offset patterns, [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) strongly opposed as out of scope for Intl. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) questioned the names `"shortGMT"` and `"longGMT"` since GMT is obsolete and not used in every locale, and [USA](../../people/USA.md) suggested `"shortOffset"`/`"longOffset"` following Temporal's terminology, which [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) backed and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) accepted. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said the arguments were compelling and would even support Stage 3. **Conclusion: consensus to seek Stage 2 (at the May plenary), with +1 from [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [ZB](../../people/ZB.md), [USA](../../people/USA.md), [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md); options renamed `shortOffset` and `longOffset`.**

## NumberFormat V3: Rounding Options Puzzle

- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#8](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/8)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked for agreement on the outcome of the April 6 deep dive: a mental model where `maximumFractionDigits`/`maximumSignificantDigits` denote the power of 10 at which to round, `roundingIncrement` supporting only 1 or 5 followed by any number of zeros, and `trailingZeroDisplay` as an orthogonal option with values `"auto"` and `"stringIfInteger"` (spelling as in the notes). [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) thanked [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) for the effort; [USA](../../people/USA.md) had no opposition and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) gave +1. **Conclusion: agreement on the rounding options (no objections).**

## NumberFormat V3: What rounding modes to include?

- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#7](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/7)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed nine rounding modes (`ceil`, `floor`, ... `halfEven`) with `halfExpand` as the default; `halfOdd` was dropped for lack of use cases. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md) asked whether the options would affect other formatters (plural rules, `RelativeTimeFormat`, [DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)); [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said other proposals can pass the options down to NumberFormat as needed and these are questions for Stage 3 review. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) gave +1; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted these were the last two open NumberFormat V3 issues. **Conclusion: agreement on the nine modes (no objections).**

## Options for smallestUnit/largestUnit and hideZeroValued #32

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#32](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/32)

[USA](../../people/USA.md) recalled last meeting's decision to move from a fields list to `smallestUnit`/`largestUnit`/`hideZeroValued`, and asked whether these could be passed to `format()` instead of only the constructor, as different durations might need different rounding. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) said the spec text lags behind the agreed changes (having prototyped three versions in V8) and would not discuss new issues until it is updated; [USA](../../people/USA.md) plans to update it within days. On the substance, [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) strongly opposed options on `format()` (formatters would need all data up front or reconstruct at format time), and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) noted the overwhelming pattern is that only the data is passed to `format`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued options that select locale data belong in the constructor because data must otherwise be prepared for every unit from nanoseconds to years, and because units are known from context (e.g. a countdown timer). **Conclusion: none recorded; [USA](../../people/USA.md) will take it offline with [FYT](../../people/FYT.md), and the options stay on the constructor per the discussion.**

## singular/plural consistency of property

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#44](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/44)

[USA](../../people/USA.md) raised the mismatch between NumberFormat's singular unit names (also `RelativeTimeFormat` `formatToParts`) and Temporal's plural. [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) argued plural would create an inconsistency in ECMA-402 and set a precedent with no plural forms elsewhere in ECMAScript; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) agreed, long favouring singular for Temporal, and did not want a Plan B unless TG1 declines. [USA](../../people/USA.md) acknowledged a strong conflict of interest as a Temporal champion and would accept singular if the group strongly favours it, though singular output with plural input seemed acceptable to [USA](../../people/USA.md). **Conclusion: TG2 will formally recommend that TG1 reconsider Temporal's plural decision ([LAF](../../people/LAF.md) +1 for singular, [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) suggests raising it beyond Temporal); CCN and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) will write it up, with [USA](../../people/USA.md) drafting the codified proposal, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) will add it to the TG1 agenda.**
