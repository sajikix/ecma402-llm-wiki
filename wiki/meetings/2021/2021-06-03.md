## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). TG2 gave consensus to four normative PRs against ECMA-402 (#571, #572, #573, #577) and to two PRs to the [Intl Locale Info](../../proposals/intl-locale-info.md) proposal (#37, #36). It agreed to drop the `region` option for time zones and discussed array versus iterator return types for Intl Enumeration. Most of the time went to [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md), where the group could not converge on what to do with duration fields that are not in the requested unit list; a straw poll was taken. In straw-poll votes, `YFT` is normalised to [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) and `ZBI` to [ZB](../../people/ZB.md). TC39's move from IRC to Matrix was announced by [LEO](../../people/LEO.md).

## Options for smallestUnit/largestUnit and hideZeroValued

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)

[JGT](../../people/JGT.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) presented proposed solutions. The group agreed not to do arithmetic on durations: [USA](../../people/USA.md) explained that rounding between, say, months and days needs a reference point that an implicit-rounding API cannot provide, and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted [DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) rounding is a developer decision while NumberFormat rounding can be locale-dependent; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) asked that this be explained editorially. The open question was what `format()` does with fields outside the constructor's unit list: [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) proposed the list as a minimal set (extra fields shown best-effort), [JGT](../../people/JGT.md) preferred throwing (supported by [RGN](../../people/RGN.md)), [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) opposed "smart" resolution in Intl, [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) leaned toward ignoring them as DateTimeFormat does, and [USA](../../people/USA.md) worried about special handling of `"digital"` and about output that silently hides information. [JGT](../../people/JGT.md) ran a straw poll with four options (best effort, drop/ignore, throw, special handling for digital); first preferences were best effort ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md), GPT, CJR tie), ignore ([ZB](../../people/ZB.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), LHO, [RCA](../../people/RCA.md)), throw ([FYT](../../people/FYT.md), [JGT](../../people/JGT.md) tie) and special handling for digital ([USA](../../people/USA.md), [JSW](../../people/JSW.md)). **Conclusion: [USA](../../people/USA.md) will continue iterating on the proposal offline.**

## Remove region option for timeZone

- proposal: [proposal-intl-enumeration#24](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-enumeration/issues/24)

Time zones are determined by location rather than by locale, so the `region` option is needed but a language is not (the language can be `und`). [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) proposed removing the option because Locale Info is now at Stage 3. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) agreed (conditional on #23, since returning arrays calls for a way to reduce their size), [USA](../../people/USA.md) expressed slight opposition, and [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) noted users can already omit the option. **Conclusion: agreement to remove the region option.**

## Returns array instead of iterator

- proposal: [proposal-intl-enumeration#23](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-enumeration/issues/23)

[FYT](../../people/FYT.md), who prototyped this in V8, argued iterators reduce memory (UTF-16 conversion); [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) preferred iterators too, since users may filter or render directly. [JSW](../../people/JSW.md), GPT, [LEO](../../people/LEO.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [YSZ](../../people/YSZ.md) and [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) were fine with or preferred arrays: arrays are simple and familiar, 5-10K of memory is small, and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) disliked introducing new iterator prototypes. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) kept a preference for iterators but would not object to arrays. **Conclusion: [FYT](../../people/FYT.md) to continue iterating offline.**
