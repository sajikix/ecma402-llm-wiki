## Overview

Co-moderated by [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) and [USA](../../people/USA.md). The meeting was a round of bikeshedding for two proposals. For [Intl.NumberFormat v3](../../proposals/intl-numberformat-v3.md), the group discussed the names and semantics of the rounding modes ([MLS](../../people/MLS.md) raised hardware and test-coverage concerns), the `"min2"` value name, a `signDisplay: "negative"` value and `roundingMode` with `-0`. For [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md), TG2 kept the `style` option name and agreed to the `smallestUnit`/`largestUnit`/`hideZeroValued` design. It also discussed whether options arguments should be required to be objects on new constructors.

## What rounding modes to include? #7

- wiki: [Intl.NumberFormat V3](../../proposals/intl-numberformat-v3.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#7](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/7)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) preferred the concise naming style of ECMA-402 (`short`, `narrow`, `long`) and so a slight preference for keeping "expand". [MLS](../../people/MLS.md) noted that hardware implements only a subset of the rounding modes, that the mode is per-process register state, and that correctness (corner cases of converting to an exact mathematical value) needs a robust test suite; [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) replied that ICU already does the rounding in software, with the ECMA-402 text citing the exact mathematical value in `ToRawFixed`. [LAF](../../people/LAF.md) noted the spec's modulo helper differs from calendrical needs. On naming, [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) suggested "outward", [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) liked "uceil" (suggested by [LEO](../../people/LEO.md)), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) knew only the precedent "away from zero", [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) suggested "bigger", and [MCM](../../people/MCM.md) was surprised the group was inventing names for terms of art; [LEO](../../people/LEO.md) suggested trusting the champions. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked whether anyone objected to adopting the 10 names listed on Oct 23; the notes record silence.

## Bikeshed: name of the "min2" option #23

- wiki: [Intl.NumberFormat V3](../../proposals/intl-numberformat-v3.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-numberformat-v3#23](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-numberformat-v3/issues/23)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md), [JSW](../../people/JSW.md) and [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) agreed `"min2"` is good. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) noted that "minimum" is spelled out elsewhere in NumberFormat and floated `"minimumTwo"`; [ZB](../../people/ZB.md) pointed out it is a value, not a key, and [JGT](../../people/JGT.md) liked having a digit as in `h12`/`h24`. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) settled back on `"min2"` and the notes record silence.

## roundingMode w/ -0 #21

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) agreed with [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) that `-0` is a variant of `0`, and [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) added that this is consistent with ECMA-262. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked for consensus on adopting the response to the issue; the notes record silence.

## Option name: style, width, or display? #34

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal-intl-duration-format#34](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format/issues/34)

[JGT](../../people/JGT.md) warned against `width` because [DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md) has a non-linear value (`"digital"`), and weakly preferred `style`, aligning with RelativeTimeFormat. [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) found `style` and `display` both poor fits but `width` unlike precedent. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) said that, starting fresh in ICU4X, they would choose `width` or `length`. **Conclusion: in ECMA-402, stick with the `style` convention (NumberFormat being the exception), and do not let this influence ICU4X; [USA](../../people/USA.md), [ZB](../../people/ZB.md), [YMD](../../people/YMD.md) and [JGT](../../people/JGT.md) agreed.**

## Require options to be an object #480

- proposal: [ecma402#480](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/issues/480)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) would rather coerce everywhere than only in new APIs, and worried that people carry habits from old APIs into new ones. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued that new APIs (which already require `new`) can be stricter to catch coding errors without breaking existing websites, since the worst outcome of the mistake is an unexpected string; [JGT](../../people/JGT.md) saw it as a validation feature. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) concluded "Okay, makes sense."; no formal conclusion is recorded.
